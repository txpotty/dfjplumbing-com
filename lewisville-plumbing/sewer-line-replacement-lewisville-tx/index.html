    Trenchless Sewer Line Repair & Replacement in Lewisville, TX

    Trenchless technology, such as pipe bursting or pipe lining, offers a less invasive alternative to traditional methods. Pipe bursting involves pulling a new pipe through the old one, fracturing the old pipe in place. Pipe lining inserts a new, durable epoxy-coated liner into the existing pipe. Both methods significantly reduce the need for extensive excavation, preserving your yard and landscaping in Lewisville, TX while providing a long-lasting solution.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Sewer Line Replacement in Lewisville

    The cost of sewer line replacement in Lewisville, TX can vary widely, typically ranging from $4,000 to $25,000 or more. We provide a detailed, upfront estimate after a thorough inspection.

    The duration depends on the complexity and method. A trenchless replacement might take 1-3 days, while a traditional trenched replacement could take 3-5 days, sometimes longer for extensive projects. We will give you a clear timeline estimate during your consultation.

    With traditional trenching, some disruption to your yard is unavoidable, but we work to minimize it and restore the area as best as possible. If trenchless methods are suitable, yard damage is significantly reduced, often limited to small access points.

    Modern sewer lines made from materials like PVC or HDPE, when properly installed, can last 50 to 100 years or even longer. This is a significant improvement over older materials like cast iron or clay, which typically have a shorter lifespan.

    Common signs include frequent sewage backups in multiple drains, gurgling noises from toilets, foul odors inside or outside your home, unusually lush or wet spots in your yard, and sudden increases in your water bill. If you notice any of these, it's time to call a professional.
