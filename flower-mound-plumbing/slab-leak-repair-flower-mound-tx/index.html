    Slab Leak Pipe Rerouting in Flower Mound

    For more complex or recurring slab leaks, or when the original piping is severely deteriorated, pipe rerouting is an excellent long-term solution. This method involves abandoning the leaky pipe under the slab and installing new plumbing lines through walls or the attic, bypassing the foundation entirely. This eliminates future slab leak risks from that particular line and is often preferred for older homes in Flower Mound with aging copper or galvanized pipes.

PEX tubing is flexible, corrosion-resistant, and less prone to leaks caused by foundation shifts, making it ideal for Texas homes.
