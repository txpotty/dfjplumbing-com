    Frequently Asked Questions About Gas Water Heater Installation in College Station

    The cost for a gas water heater installation in College Station typically ranges from $1,200 to $3,000, depending on the unit size, type (tank vs. tankless), and any necessary gas line or venting modifications. We provide a detailed, upfront quote after a site assessment.

    A conventional gas water heater stores and heats a large volume of water in a tank, providing a limited supply. A gas tankless water heater heats water on demand as it flows through the unit, offering an endless supply but requiring more complex venting and gas line infrastructure.

    We install all major reputable brands of gas water heaters, including Rheem, Bradford White, A. O. Smith, State Water Heaters, and Navien for tankless models. We can help you choose the best brand and model for your specific needs and budget.

    Most standard gas water heater installations take between 3 to 5 hours. More complex installations, such as converting to a tankless unit or significant gas line adjustments, might take a full day. We work efficiently to minimize disruption to your home.

    Most new gas water heaters come with a manufacturer's warranty ranging from 6 to 12 years on the tank and components. DFJ Plumbing also provides a warranty on our installation labor. We will provide full details on both during your consultation.

    Installing a gas water heater involves working with natural gas lines, water lines, and exhaust venting, which can be dangerous if not done correctly. Improper installation can lead to gas leaks, carbon monoxide poisoning, or water damage. It is strongly recommended to hire a licensed professional for safety and code compliance.

    Regular maintenance, such as annual flushing of the tank to remove sediment and checking the anode rod, can significantly extend your gas water heater's lifespan. Our team can advise on best practices and offer maintenance services.
