    Frequently Asked Questions About Residential Plumbing Service in McAllen

    The cost for residential plumbing service in McAllen varies widely depending on the type and complexity of the job. Major projects like slab leak repair or whole-home repiping can cost several thousand dollars. We always provide a detailed, upfront quote after diagnosing the issue.

    A slab leak is a leak in the water pipes located beneath your home's concrete foundation. They are common in Texas due to shifting clay soils, which can put stress on pipes, and the prevalence of homes built on slab foundations. Signs include warm spots on the floor, sudden increases in water bills, or the sound of running water when no fixtures are in use.

    For significant plumbing work like water heater replacement, sewer line repair, or new gas line installations in McAllen, TX, a permit is typically required by local code. DFJ Plumbing handles all necessary permit applications and ensures all work complies with city regulations, saving you time and hassle.

    For plumbing emergencies in McAllen, TX, we prioritize rapid response. Our goal is to have a licensed plumber at your home as quickly as possible, often within a few hours, to mitigate damage. We understand the urgency of situations like burst pipes or severe overflows and act swiftly.

    To prevent burst pipes during a Texas freeze, insulate exposed pipes, especially those in unheated areas like attics or crawl spaces. Disconnect garden hoses, open cabinet doors under sinks to allow warm air in, and consider letting faucets drip slowly. If you're leaving your McAllen home, consider shutting off the main water supply and draining your pipes.

    Hard water contains high levels of dissolved minerals, primarily calcium and magnesium, common in McAllen's water supply. Soft water has these minerals removed. Hard water can cause mineral buildup in pipes, reduced soap lather, and appliance damage, while soft water is gentler on plumbing and skin.

    We recommend having your water heater inspected annually, especially in McAllen where hard water can accelerate sediment buildup. Regular inspections can extend the lifespan of your unit, improve efficiency, and help identify potential issues like anode rod corrosion or pressure relief valve problems before they become major repairs.

    Common signs that you need professional drain cleaning include slow drains, gurgling sounds from your pipes, foul odors coming from drains, and water backing up in sinks or showers.
