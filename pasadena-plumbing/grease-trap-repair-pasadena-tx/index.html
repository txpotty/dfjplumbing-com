    Leaking Grease Trap Repair in Pasadena, TX

    A leaking grease trap can cause significant property damage, foul odors, and potential health code violations. Leaks often stem from corroded tanks, cracked seams, or failing pipe connections. We use leak detection techniques to pinpoint the source and provide targeted repairs, which may involve patching the tank, sealing connections, or replacing damaged sections of the trap or associated plumbing.     Damaged Baffle Repair in Pasadena

    The baffles inside a grease trap are critical for separating FOGs from wastewater. Our service includes inspecting the condition of all baffles and repairing or replacing them as needed, often using durable, corrosion-resistant materials. Proper baffle function is key to compliance with wastewater regulations in Pasadena.

    The cost of grease trap repair in Pasadena, TX can vary widely, typically ranging from $300 for minor clogs or baffle repairs to over $2,000 for extensive damage, tank replacement, or emergency services requiring hydro-jetting. Proper repair and maintenance are crucial for environmental protection.

    While minor clogs might seem DIY-fixable, grease trap repair is generally best left to licensed plumbers. These systems involve complex plumbing, potential health hazards from wastewater, and strict local codes in Texas.     Key signs include slow drainage in your kitchen sinks, foul odors emanating from drains or the trap itself, visible FOGs in the wastewater leaving the trap, or an overflow. If you notice any of these, it's time to call a professional for an inspection to prevent further damage or violations.

    Our plumbers are experienced in repairing all major brands and types of commercial grease traps and interceptors, including but not limited to Zurn, Big Dipper, Schier, Proceptor, and Rockford Separators.     To prevent future problems, implement strict FOG management practices, including scraping plates into the trash before washing, regularly cleaning your grease trap (typically every 1-3 months depending on usage), and avoiding pouring grease down drains.
