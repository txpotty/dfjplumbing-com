    Frequently Asked Questions About Sewer Cleaning in Pasadena

    The cost of sewer cleaning in Pasadena, TX can range from $200 for a minor clog cleared with a snake to $600 or more for complex hydro jetting or root removal.     Most standard sewer cleaning jobs in Pasadena can be completed within 1 to 3 hours, depending on the severity and nature of the clog. More complex issues, like extensive root removal or if a camera inspection is needed first, might take longer. We'll provide an estimated timeline during our initial assessment.

    Drain cleaning typically refers to clearing clogs in smaller branch lines leading from individual fixtures, like sinks or tubs. Sewer cleaning, on the other hand, addresses blockages in the main sewer line that collects wastewater from all household drains and carries it to the municipal system. Sewer clogs affect all drains, while drain clogs are usually isolated.

    Common signs include multiple drains backing up simultaneously, gurgling noises from toilets or drains, foul odors coming from drains, and water pooling in your yard, especially near the sewer line. If you notice any of these in your Pasadena home, it's time to call a professional.

    If our sewer cleaning process or camera inspection reveals damage like cracks, collapses, or severe root intrusion that cannot be resolved by cleaning alone, DFJ Plumbing will provide you with a detailed assessment and options for sewer line repair or replacement.     For standard sewer cleaning, such as snaking or hydro jetting to clear a blockage, a permit is typically not required in Pasadena. We handle all necessary permits for larger jobs.

    Generally, standard home insurance policies in Texas do not cover routine sewer cleaning or damage from gradual wear and tear.     For most homes in Pasadena, an annual or bi-annual sewer cleaning is sufficient for preventative maintenance, especially if you have older pipes or mature trees nearby.
