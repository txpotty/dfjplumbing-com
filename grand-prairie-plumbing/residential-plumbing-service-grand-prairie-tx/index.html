    Common signs that you need professional drain cleaning include slow drains, gurgling sounds from your pipes, foul odors coming from drains, and water backing up in sinks or showers.     Hard water contains high levels of dissolved minerals, primarily calcium and magnesium, common in Grand Prairie's water supply. Soft water has these minerals removed. Hard water can cause mineral buildup in pipes, reduced soap lather, and appliance damage, while soft water is gentler on plumbing and skin.

    We recommend having your water heater inspected annually, especially in Grand Prairie where hard water can accelerate sediment buildup. Regular inspections can extend the lifespan of your unit, improve efficiency, and help identify potential issues like anode rod corrosion or pressure relief valve problems before they become major repairs.

    To prevent burst pipes during a Texas freeze, insulate exposed pipes, especially those in unheated areas like attics or crawl spaces.
