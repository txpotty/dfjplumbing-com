    The cost of drain unblocking in Tyler, TX can vary widely depending on the severity and location of the clog.     Most standard drain unblocking jobs, like a single sink or toilet clog, can be completed within 1 to 2 hours. More complex issues, such as main sewer line blockages requiring camera inspection or hydro jetting, may take 3 to 5 hours. We always aim for efficient service.

    While chemical drain cleaners are widely available, DFJ Plumbing strongly advises against using them. They often contain harsh chemicals that can damage your pipes, especially older ones, and may not fully clear the clog. Professional tools are safer and more effective for long-term solutions.

    DFJ Plumbing uses a range of professional-grade equipment from trusted manufacturers for our drain services.     For routine drain unblocking or simple repairs, a permit is typically not required.
