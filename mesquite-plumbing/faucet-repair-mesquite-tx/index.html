    Cartridge Faucet Repair in Mesquite, TX

    Many modern single-handle faucets, commonly found in newer Texas homes, use a cartridge system. When these fail, you might experience inconsistent water temperature, reduced flow, or difficulty turning the faucet on or off. Our technicians are experts at diagnosing cartridge issues and replacing them with the correct manufacturer-specific part, ensuring your faucet functions like new. We work with all major brands to provide precise repairs.

    Compression Faucet Repair in Mesquite

    Compression faucets, often found in older Mesquite homes, are known for their separate hot and cold handles. When these faucets leak, it's typically due to worn-out rubber washers or packing. Our team is skilled in disassembling these classic fixtures, replacing the necessary seals and washers, and reassembling them to stop leaks effectively. We respect the character of older homes and ensure these repairs are done carefully.

    Ball Faucet Repair in Mesquite, TX

    Ball faucets, recognizable by their single lever that moves over a rounded cap, are common in many Texas kitchens. They use a rotating ball joint to control water flow and temperature. If your ball faucet is leaking from the spout or base, or if the handle feels loose, it's likely time for a repair. We can replace the spring-loaded rubber seats and O-rings, which are usually the culprits, restoring smooth operation and preventing drips.

    Disc Faucet Repair in Mesquite

    Ceramic disc faucets are known for their durability and are a popular choice in many contemporary Mesquite bathrooms. While generally reliable, they can develop leaks if the ceramic discs become scratched or misaligned, or if the inlet and outlet seals wear out. Our plumbers have the expertise to carefully disassemble these precise mechanisms, clean or replace the ceramic discs, and reseal the faucet to eliminate leaks and restore optimal performance.
