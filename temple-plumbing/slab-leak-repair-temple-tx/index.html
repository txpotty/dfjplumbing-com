    Electronic Slab Leak Detection in Temple

    Locating a hidden slab leak requires specialized tools. Our plumbers use advanced electronic listening devices and thermal imaging cameras to pinpoint the exact location of the leak under your concrete foundation. This precision detection minimizes the need for extensive concrete removal, saving you time and money. We can detect leaks in both hot and cold water lines, as well as sewer lines, ensuring a comprehensive diagnosis for your Temple home.

    Direct Slab Leak Repair in Temple, TX

    When a slab leak is localized and accessible, direct repair is often the most efficient solution. This involves carefully jackhammering a small section of your foundation to expose the damaged pipe, then repairing or replacing the compromised section. Our technicians are skilled in precise concrete cutting and restoration, ensuring the repair is effective and the area is professionally restored. We use durable materials designed to withstand the expansive clay soils common in Texas.

    Slab Leak Pipe Rerouting in Temple

    For more complex or recurring slab leaks, or when the original piping is severely deteriorated, pipe rerouting is an excellent long-term solution. This method involves abandoning the leaky pipe under the slab and installing new plumbing lines through walls or the attic, bypassing the foundation entirely. This eliminates future slab leak risks from that particular line and is often preferred for older homes in Temple with aging copper or galvanized pipes.

    PEX Repiping for Slab Leaks in Temple, TX

    If multiple slab leaks indicate a systemic issue with your home's original plumbing, a full PEX repipe might be the most cost-effective and durable solution. PEX tubing is flexible, corrosion-resistant, and less prone to leaks caused by foundation shifts, making it ideal for Texas homes. We can replace all the water supply lines in your Temple home, providing a brand-new, leak-free plumbing system that will last for decades.
