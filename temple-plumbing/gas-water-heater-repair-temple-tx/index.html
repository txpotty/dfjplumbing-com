    Frequently Asked Questions About Gas Water Heater Repair in Temple

    The cost of gas water heater repair in Temple, TX can vary widely depending on the issue. Minor repairs like a thermocouple replacement might range from $150-$350, while more complex issues such as a gas valve replacement could be $400-$800 or more. We always provide a detailed, upfront estimate after diagnosis.

    Due to the inherent risks associated with natural gas and potential carbon monoxide poisoning, gas water heater repairs should always be handled by a licensed professional. DIY attempts can be dangerous and may void warranties or violate local Temple, TX plumbing codes.

    Most common gas water heater repairs can be completed within 1 to 3 hours. However, more complex issues or if special parts need to be ordered could extend the timeline. We strive for efficiency without compromising safety or quality.

    Generally, minor repairs like replacing a thermocouple or a thermostat do not require a permit. However, if the repair involves significant alterations to the gas line or venting system, or if the unit needs to be replaced, a permit from the Temple building department might be necessary. We handle all necessary permitting.

    If you smell gas, immediately evacuate your home, do not turn on or off any lights or appliances, and call your gas company's emergency line and then DFJ Plumbing from a safe location. This indicates a potentially dangerous gas leak that requires immediate professional attention.

    Look out for a lack of hot water, inconsistent water temperature, a pilot light that won't stay lit, unusual noises like rumbling or popping, a rotten egg smell (indicating a gas leak), or visible leaks around the tank. Any of these warrant a professional inspection.
