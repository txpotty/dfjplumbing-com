    Signs of a failing expansion tank include water dripping from the tank's air valve, rust or corrosion on the tank's exterior, or your water heater frequently cycling on and off. You might also notice fluctuating water pressure or a banging sound in your pipes, indicating excessive pressure build-up.

    When water heats up, it expands. Without an expansion tank, this expanded water has nowhere to go, increasing pressure within your closed plumbing system. This constant pressure can prematurely wear out water heater components, relief valves, and even cause leaks or ruptures. The expansion tank absorbs this excess volume, protecting your entire system.
