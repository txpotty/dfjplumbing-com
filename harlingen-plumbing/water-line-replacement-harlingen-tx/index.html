    Copper has long been a preferred material for water lines due to its durability, corrosion resistance, and longevity.     PEX Water Line Installation in Harlingen, TX

PEX is also freeze-resistant, expanding and contracting without bursting, which is a significant advantage during occasional cold snaps in Harlingen. Its flexibility allows for fewer fittings and faster installation.
