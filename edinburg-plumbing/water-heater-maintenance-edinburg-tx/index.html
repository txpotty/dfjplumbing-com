    Our standard tank water heater maintenance includes flushing the tank to remove sediment buildup, which is common with hard water in many parts of Texas.     Tankless Water Heater Maintenance in Edinburg, TX

    Tankless water heaters require specialized maintenance, primarily involving a descaling flush to remove mineral deposits that can restrict flow and reduce efficiency. Our technicians in Edinburg, TX use commercial-grade solutions to clean the heat exchanger, ensuring your unit continues to provide endless hot water efficiently.     Electric Water Heater Tune-Up in Edinburg

    For electric water heaters, our maintenance service focuses on checking heating elements, thermostats, and electrical connections.     Gas Water Heater Safety Checks in Edinburg, TX

    Gas water heater maintenance involves crucial safety inspections in Edinburg, TX. We check for gas leaks, proper ventilation of exhaust gases, and the functionality of the pilot light or igniter.     Water Heater Anode Rod Inspection in Edinburg

    The anode rod is a sacrificial component that protects your water heater tank from corrosion. During maintenance in Edinburg, we inspect the condition of the anode rod and recommend replacement if it's significantly depleted. Replacing a worn anode rod can extend the life of your water heater by several years, preventing costly tank leaks.
