    Most standard leak detection services can be completed within 1 to 3 hours. More complex cases, such as multiple leaks or hard-to-access areas like under a slab, might take longer to accurately pinpoint.

    We use a range of advanced tools including acoustic listening devices, thermal imaging cameras, specialized moisture meters, pressure testing equipment, and tracer gas detectors to ensure accurate and non-invasive leak identification.

    While you can check for obvious leaks like dripping faucets, hidden leaks require professional tools and expertise. DIY attempts can often misdiagnose the problem or cause further damage, especially with slab or gas leaks.
