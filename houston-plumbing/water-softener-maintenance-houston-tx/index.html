    Routine Water Softener Tune-Ups in Houston

    Our routine tune-ups involve a comprehensive inspection of your entire water softener system.     Brine Tank Cleaning and Inspection in Houston, TX

    Over time, sludge and insoluble materials can accumulate in your brine tank, hindering the softening process. Our maintenance includes thoroughly cleaning the brine tank, inspecting for any cracks or leaks, and ensuring the float valve operates correctly. This step is crucial for the efficient regeneration of your water softener in Houston, TX.

    Resin Bed Analysis and Regeneration in Houston

    The resin bed is the heart of your water softener, where ion exchange occurs. During maintenance, we analyze the condition of the resin beads and ensure the regeneration cycles are programmed correctly for your household's water usage. Proper resin bed function is key to consistent soft water throughout your home in Houston.

    Valve and Controller Diagnostics in Houston, TX

    The control valve and electronic controller regulate the entire softening process.
