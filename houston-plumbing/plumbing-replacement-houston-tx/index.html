    Water Heater Replacement in Houston

    When your water heater is leaking, not heating, or providing insufficient hot water, it is often more economical to replace it than to repair it, especially if it is over 10 years old. We install standard tank water heaters, ensuring proper sizing for your home's needs and compliance with Texas energy efficiency standards. Our team handles the safe removal of your old unit and the precise installation of your new one, connecting gas or electric lines, and ensuring correct venting and pressure relief valve operation for optimal performance and safety.

    Tankless Water Heater Replacement in Houston, TX

    Upgrade to endless hot water with a tankless water heater replacement. These units heat water on demand, saving energy and space, which is a great benefit for Houston homes. We specialize in replacing older tankless units or converting from traditional tank heaters to high-efficiency tankless models from brands like Rinnai and Noritz. Our service includes assessing your home's gas line capacity or electrical service, ensuring proper venting, and precise installation to maximize efficiency and longevity.

    Sewer Line Replacement in Houston

    Persistent sewer backups, foul odors, or lush spots in your yard can signal a failing sewer line, a common issue in Houston due to shifting clay soils. We offer comprehensive sewer line replacement using trenchless technology whenever possible, minimizing disruption to your landscaping. For more severe damage, traditional excavation is performed with meticulous care. We ensure new lines are properly graded, connected, and meet all local Houston plumbing codes, preventing future blockages and costly repairs.

    Water Line Replacement in Houston, TX

    A leaking main water line can lead to significant water loss, high bills, and foundation damage, which is a serious concern for homes on slab foundations in Texas. Our water line replacement services address issues like corroded pipes, leaks, or low water pressure. We locate the leak, excavate the area if necessary, and replace the damaged section or the entire line with durable PEX or copper piping. We ensure a secure, leak-free connection from the meter to your home, restoring reliable water flow.

    Well Pump & Pressure Tank Replacement in Houston

    For rural properties in and around Houston that rely on well water, a failing well pump or pressure tank can lead to no water at all. We diagnose issues with your current well system and provide expert replacement services for submersible or jet pumps. We also replace faulty pressure tanks, ensuring your well system maintains consistent water pressure and extends the life of your pump. Proper sizing and installation are crucial for efficient operation and reliable water supply.

    Water Softener Replacement in Houston, TX

    Hard water is common in many parts of Texas, leading to mineral buildup in pipes and appliances. If your old water softener is no longer effectively removing minerals, or if it is over 15 years old, a replacement will protect your plumbing and improve water quality. We replace all types of water softeners, including salt-based and salt-free systems, ensuring proper sizing for your household's water usage and correct installation into your main water line to provide truly soft water throughout your Houston home.

    Gas Line Replacement in Houston

    Old or damaged gas lines pose a serious safety risk and require immediate attention. If you suspect a gas leak or if your gas lines are showing signs of corrosion or age, our certified plumbers in Houston can perform safe and code-compliant gas line replacement. We use appropriate materials like black steel pipe or corrugated stainless steel tubing (CSST) for secure installations, ensuring all connections are leak-tested and meet strict safety regulations for your home's protection.

    Pipe Replacement & Whole-Home Repiping in Houston, TX

    Homes in Houston, TX with older galvanized steel or polybutylene pipes often experience low water pressure, discolored water, or frequent leaks. A whole-home repipe with new PEX or copper piping can dramatically improve water quality and pressure, and eliminate leak concerns.
