    Our standard tank water heater maintenance includes flushing the tank to remove sediment buildup, which is common with hard water in many parts of Texas.     Tankless Water Heater Maintenance in El Paso, TX

    Tankless water heaters require specialized maintenance, primarily involving a descaling flush to remove mineral deposits that can restrict flow and reduce efficiency. Our technicians in El Paso, TX use commercial-grade solutions to clean the heat exchanger, ensuring your unit continues to provide endless hot water efficiently.     Electric Water Heater Tune-Up in El Paso

    For electric water heaters, our maintenance service focuses on checking heating elements, thermostats, and electrical connections.     Gas Water Heater Safety Checks in El Paso, TX

    Gas water heater maintenance involves crucial safety inspections in El Paso, TX. We check for gas leaks, proper ventilation of exhaust gases, and the functionality of the pilot light or igniter. We also clean the burner assembly and inspect the thermocouple. These checks are vital to prevent carbon monoxide exposure and ensure the safe operation of your gas-fired appliance.

    Water Heater Anode Rod Inspection in El Paso

    The anode rod is a sacrificial component that protects your water heater tank from corrosion. Replacing a worn anode rod can extend the life of your water heater by several years, preventing costly tank leaks.
