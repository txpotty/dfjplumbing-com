    Frequently Asked Questions About Electric Tankless Water Heater Installation in El Paso

    The cost for electric tankless water heater installation in El Paso, TX typically ranges from $2,500 to $6,000.     With proper installation and regular maintenance, an electric tankless water heater can last 20 years or more, which is significantly longer than the 10-15 year lifespan of a traditional tank unit. Their modular design also means components can often be replaced, extending their service life further.

    Texas is known for its hard water, which can lead to mineral buildup (scale) inside electric tankless water heaters. This can reduce efficiency and lifespan. Regular flushing and descaling, often recommended annually, are crucial maintenance steps to combat hard water effects and keep your unit running optimally.

    Installing an electric tankless water heater is a complex job involving high-voltage electricity and plumbing, making it unsuitable for DIY. Incorrect installation can lead to electrical hazards, water damage, and voided warranties. It's always best to hire a licensed and experienced plumber like DFJ Plumbing for safe and proper installation.

    Yes, in El Paso and most of Texas, permits are typically required for electric tankless water heater installation, especially due to the electrical work involved. DFJ Plumbing handles all necessary permit applications and ensures that the installation passes all required inspections, guaranteeing compliance with local building codes.

    Most electric tankless water heaters come with a manufacturer's warranty, typically 5-10 years on the heat exchanger and 1-3 years on parts. DFJ Plumbing also provides a labor warranty on our installation services. We will discuss specific warranty details for your chosen unit during your consultation.
