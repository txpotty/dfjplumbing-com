    Video Camera Sewer Inspection in El Paso

    Before any repair, we utilize advanced video camera inspection to accurately locate and assess sewer line damage. This involves inserting a flexible rod with a high-resolution camera into your sewer line, allowing us to see blockages, cracks, root intrusion, or pipe collapses in real-time. This non-invasive method prevents unnecessary digging and helps us provide a precise diagnosis and repair plan. It's crucial for understanding issues unique to older homes in El Paso and identifying problems like bellied pipes or collapsed sections.

    Trenchless Sewer Line Repair in El Paso, TX

    For many sewer line issues, especially those caused by tree roots or pipe corrosion, trenchless repair offers a less invasive solution. This method, which includes pipe bursting or pipe lining, requires minimal excavation, saving your yard and landscaping from extensive disruption. We use specialized equipment to either pull a new pipe through the old one or insert a resin-coated liner that cures in place, creating a new, durable pipe inside the old one. It's an efficient and popular choice for maintaining property aesthetics in El Paso.

    Traditional Sewer Line Replacement in El Paso

    When trenchless repair isn't feasible due to severe damage or specific pipe configurations, traditional dig-and-replace methods are necessary. This involves excavating a trench to access and replace the damaged section of the sewer line. While more intrusive, it is sometimes the only way to address severely collapsed pipes, significant offsets, or certain types of pipe materials common in older Texas properties. Our team ensures careful excavation and efficient replacement, minimizing the impact on your property in El Paso.

    Sewer Pipe Relining in El Paso, TX

    Sewer pipe relining is a trenchless repair technique where a new, durable pipe is created within the existing damaged one. A flexible, epoxy-impregnated liner is inserted into the old pipe and inflated, curing in place to form a seamless, strong, and corrosion-resistant new pipe. This method is effective for addressing cracks, minor breaks, and root intrusion without extensive digging. It extends the life of your sewer system and is an excellent option for preserving landscapes in El Paso.
