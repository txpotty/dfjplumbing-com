    Trenchless Sewer Line Repair & Replacement in Atascocita, TX

    Trenchless technology, such as pipe bursting or pipe lining, offers a less invasive alternative to traditional methods. Pipe bursting involves pulling a new pipe through the old one, fracturing the old pipe in place. Pipe lining inserts a new, durable epoxy-coated liner into the existing pipe.
