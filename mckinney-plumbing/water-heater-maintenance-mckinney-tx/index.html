    Tankless Water Heater Maintenance in McKinney, TX

    Tankless water heaters require specialized maintenance, primarily involving a descaling flush to remove mineral deposits that can restrict flow and reduce efficiency.     Gas Water Heater Safety Checks in McKinney, TX
