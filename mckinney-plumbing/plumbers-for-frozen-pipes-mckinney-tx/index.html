    To prevent future freezing in McKinney, insulate exposed pipes, especially those in attics, crawl spaces, and exterior walls. Disconnect garden hoses and drain outdoor spigots.     While no pipe is completely freeze-proof, certain materials like PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) are more flexible and resistant to bursting when frozen compared to rigid copper or PVC.     Texas plumbing codes, while generally less stringent on freeze protection than northern states, do require proper insulation for pipes in unconditioned spaces and minimum burial depths for water lines to reduce freezing risk.     The time it takes to thaw a frozen pipe varies significantly. A small, easily accessible section might thaw in 30 minutes with professional tools.
