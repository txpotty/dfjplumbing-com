Regular maintenance helps mitigate these effects.

    While you can perform basic checks like ensuring the pump is plugged in and the discharge pipe isn't blocked, professional maintenance goes much deeper. Our technicians can identify subtle electrical issues, float switch malfunctions, or impeller damage that a DIY check might miss. We recommend professional service for thoroughness and safety.

    DFJ Plumbing services all major brands of sump pumps common in McKinney homes, including Zoeller, Wayne, Little Giant, Liberty Pumps, and Flotec. Our technicians are trained on the specifics of various models, ensuring expert care regardless of your pump's manufacturer.

    We recommend having your sump pump professionally maintained at least once a year. If your home is in an area prone to heavy rainfall or has a high water table, such as many parts of McKinney, bi-annual maintenance might be more beneficial. Regular checks ensure it's ready for peak seasons.

    The check valve is crucial because it prevents water that has been pumped out from flowing back into the sump pit after the pump shuts off.
