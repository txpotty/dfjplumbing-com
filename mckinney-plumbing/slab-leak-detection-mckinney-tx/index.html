    Electronic Acoustic Leak Detection in McKinney

    Our team uses advanced electronic listening devices to pinpoint the exact location of a slab leak.     Thermal Imaging for Slab Leaks in McKinney, TX

    Thermal imaging cameras allow us to visualize temperature differences on surfaces, which can indicate a hidden water leak. As water escapes a hot water line, it creates a distinct thermal signature on the concrete slab. This technique is particularly useful for hot water line leaks and helps us narrow down the search area significantly without any physical disruption to your property in McKinney.

    Pressure Testing & Isolation in McKinney

    When a leak is suspected, we can perform pressure tests on individual water lines. By isolating sections of your plumbing system and monitoring pressure gauges, we can confirm the presence of a leak and often determine which specific line is compromised.     Video Pipe Inspection for Sewer Leaks in McKinney, TX

    While less common for slab leaks, sewer line issues under a slab can also mimic slab leak symptoms.
