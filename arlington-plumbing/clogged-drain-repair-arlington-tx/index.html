    You should call a professional plumber for clogged drain repair if DIY methods like plungers are ineffective, if you experience multiple clogged drains, or if you suspect a main sewer line blockage.     Common signs of a clogged drain include slow drainage, gurgling sounds from drains or toilets, unpleasant odors coming from your pipes, and water backing up into sinks, tubs, or showers. If multiple drains are affected, it often indicates a main line issue.

    While chemical drain cleaners are readily available, DFJ Plumbing generally advises against them for homes in Arlington.
