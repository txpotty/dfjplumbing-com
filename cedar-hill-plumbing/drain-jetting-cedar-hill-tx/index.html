    Drain snaking typically involves pushing an auger through a clog to create a small opening for water to pass. Jetting is more effective for stubborn blockages and preventative maintenance.

    For most homes, preventative drain jetting every 1-2 years is sufficient, especially if you experience frequent minor clogs. Businesses, particularly those in food service, may benefit from quarterly or semi-annual jetting to prevent grease buildup. We can recommend a schedule based on your specific needs.

    We strongly advise against using a home pressure washer for drain jetting. These machines lack the necessary power, specialized nozzles, and safety features of professional equipment. Improper use can damage your pipes, cause severe injury, or push clogs deeper. Always rely on trained professionals for drain jetting.

    Generally, a permit is not required for routine drain jetting services as it is a maintenance activity within existing plumbing.
