    When water heats up, it expands. Without an expansion tank, this expanded water has nowhere to go, increasing pressure within your closed plumbing system. This constant pressure can prematurely wear out water heater components, relief valves, and even cause leaks or ruptures. The expansion tank absorbs this excess volume, protecting your entire system.

    In Texas, especially in areas like Cedar Hill, an expansion tank is crucial because water expands when heated. Without a tank to absorb this expansion, the increased pressure can damage your water heater, pipes, and fixtures.     Signs of a failing expansion tank include water dripping from the tank's air valve, rust or corrosion on the tank's exterior, or your water heater frequently cycling on and off.
