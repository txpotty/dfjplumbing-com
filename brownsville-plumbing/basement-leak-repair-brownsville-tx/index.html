    Exterior Waterproofing Solutions in Brownsville, TX

    For comprehensive basement leak prevention, exterior waterproofing involves excavating around your foundation and applying waterproof membranes or coatings.     Interior Drainage Systems in Brownsville

    When exterior excavation isn't feasible, interior drainage systems provide an effective solution for managing basement leaks. This system is less disruptive and can be a cost-effective way to dry out a wet basement.
