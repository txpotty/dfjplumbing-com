    Sump Pump vs. Sewage Ejector Pump Installation in Allen

    While both sump pumps and sewage ejector pumps are used to remove water, their applications are distinct. DFJ Plumbing provides expert guidance and installation for both in Allen. A sump pump handles groundwater and storm runoff, typically in a basement's lowest point to prevent flooding. A sewage ejector pump, however, is designed specifically for wastewater containing solids from toilets, sinks, and showers in below-grade plumbing. We ensure you get the correct pump for your specific drainage needs, preventing costly misinstallations.
