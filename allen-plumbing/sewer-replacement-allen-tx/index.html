    Traditional Trench Sewer Replacement in Allen

    For severely damaged or collapsed sewer lines, traditional trenching is often the most effective solution. This involves excavating a trench along the sewer line's path to allow for direct access and replacement of the entire pipe. While more invasive, it provides a complete overhaul and is often necessary for older homes with clay or cast iron pipes that have reached the end of their lifespan. We ensure careful planning and execution to minimize disruption to your yard.

    Trenchless Sewer Repair Options in Allen, TX

    Where applicable, we offer trenchless sewer repair methods like pipe bursting. This technique involves pulling a new pipe through the old one, simultaneously breaking the existing pipe apart. It's a less disruptive option that requires only two small access points, saving your landscaping and driveway from extensive excavation. Trenchless methods are ideal for certain types of pipe damage and property layouts in Allen.

    Sewer Line Material Upgrades in Allen

    Many older homes in Allen still have original clay or cast iron sewer lines, which are prone to root intrusion, corrosion, and cracking.
