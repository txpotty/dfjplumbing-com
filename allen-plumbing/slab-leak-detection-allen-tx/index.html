    Our team uses advanced electronic listening devices to pinpoint the exact location of a slab leak.     Thermal Imaging for Slab Leaks in Allen, TX

    Thermal imaging cameras allow us to visualize temperature differences on surfaces, which can indicate a hidden water leak. As water escapes a hot water line, it creates a distinct thermal signature on the concrete slab. This technique is particularly useful for hot water line leaks and helps us narrow down the search area significantly without any physical disruption to your property in Allen.

    Pressure Testing & Isolation in Allen

    When a leak is suspected, we can perform pressure tests on individual water lines.
