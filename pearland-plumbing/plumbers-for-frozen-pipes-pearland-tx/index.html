    The time it takes to thaw a frozen pipe varies significantly. A small, easily accessible section might thaw in 30 minutes with professional tools.     DFJ Plumbing stands behind our frozen pipe repairs in Pearland. We offer a comprehensive warranty on both parts and labor, typically for one year, ensuring that the repair holds up. We use high-quality materials and professional techniques to provide lasting solutions and peace of mind.

    While no pipe is completely freeze-proof, certain materials like PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) are more flexible and resistant to bursting when frozen compared to rigid copper or PVC.     Common signs of a frozen pipe include a sudden drop in water pressure, no water coming from a specific faucet, visible frost on an exposed pipe, or strange gurgling sounds from your drains. If a pipe has burst, you might see water stains, hear dripping, or notice a sudden increase in your water bill.

    Texas plumbing codes, while generally less stringent on freeze protection than northern states, do require proper insulation for pipes in unconditioned spaces and minimum burial depths for water lines to reduce freezing risk.
