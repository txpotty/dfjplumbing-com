    For high-hazard applications in Pearland, TX where toxic substances could contaminate the water supply, a Reduced Pressure Zone (RPZ) device is often required. RPZ backflow preventers offer the highest level of protection, utilizing a relief valve to discharge water if the pressure drops. DFJ Plumbing specializes in the complex installation of RPZ devices, ensuring all components are correctly sized, positioned, and calibrated according to strict state and local plumbing codes.
