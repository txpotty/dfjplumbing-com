    Frequently Asked Questions About Water Heater Replacement in Pearland

    The cost of water heater replacement in Pearland, TX typically ranges from $1,200 to $3,500, depending on the type of unit (gas, electric, tankless, or hybrid), its capacity, and any necessary plumbing modifications. Our team will provide a detailed, upfront quote after assessing your specific needs and existing setup.

    Most standard tank-style water heater replacements can be completed in 3 to 5 hours, assuming no major complications or plumbing reconfigurations. Tankless or hybrid installations might take a bit longer, often 5 to 8 hours, due to more complex venting or gas line adjustments.

    Common signs include a lack of hot water, rust-colored water, metallic taste in hot water, strange noises like rumbling or popping from the tank, visible leaks around the base of the unit, or an old water heater that is over 10-12 years old. If you notice any of these, it's time to call a professional.

    A traditional water heater stores and heats a limited amount of water in a tank, whereas a tankless water heater heats water on demand as it flows through the unit. Tankless units offer endless hot water and can be more energy-efficient, but often have a higher upfront cost and may require gas line upgrades.

    Most new water heaters come with a manufacturer's warranty ranging from 6 to 12 years on the tank and components, depending on the model and brand. DFJ Plumbing also provides a labor warranty on our installation services, ensuring your investment is protected.

    DFJ Plumbing installs and services all major brands, including Bradford White, Rheem, AO Smith, Rinnai, Navien, and State Water Heaters. We can help you choose the best brand and model that fits your budget and hot water demands.

    Yes, in many areas of Pearland and Texas, a plumbing permit is required for water heater replacement to ensure the installation meets local safety and building codes. DFJ Plumbing handles all necessary permitting processes on your behalf, so you don't have to worry about compliance.

    In Texas, due to varying water hardness, it's recommended to flush your traditional tank water heater annually. This helps remove sediment buildup, which can reduce efficiency and shorten the lifespan of the unit. Regular flushing can also prevent scale from impacting heating elements.
