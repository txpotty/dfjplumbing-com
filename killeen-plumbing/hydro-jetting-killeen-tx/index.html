    For most homes in Killeen, hydro jetting every 1 to 3 years can be a good preventative measure, especially if you experience recurring drain issues or have older pipes. Businesses, particularly restaurants, may benefit from quarterly or semi-annual hydro jetting to manage grease buildup. We can recommend a schedule based on your specific needs.

    Hydro jetting is exceptionally effective against almost all types of clogs. It can break through solid obstructions like tree roots, flush out heavy grease and food buildup, clear mineral scale from hard water, and remove compacted sludge and debris that traditional snaking often leaves behind.

    A standard residential hydro jetting service in Killeen typically takes between 1 to 3 hours. This includes the initial camera inspection, the jetting process itself, and a final inspection. More extensive commercial jobs or severe main line blockages might require more time.

    While small, low-pressure drain jetters are available for rent, professional hydro jetting equipment is significantly more powerful and requires specialized training to operate safely and effectively.     Key signs you might need hydro jetting include frequently recurring clogs in the same drain, multiple slow drains throughout your home, persistent foul odors coming from drains, or gurgling sounds from toilets or drains.
