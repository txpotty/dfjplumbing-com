    Snaking involves using an auger to punch a hole through a clog, often leaving behind some debris. Hydrojetting, on the other hand, uses high-pressure water to thoroughly scour the entire inner surface of the pipe, removing all buildup and leaving it completely clean. Hydrojetting provides a much more comprehensive and longer-lasting solution.

    Yes, hydrojet plumbing is highly effective at clearing tree roots from sewer lines. We use specialized nozzles designed to cut through even dense root masses.     While hydrojetting provides a thorough clean, some underlying issues like severely damaged or collapsed pipes might cause clogs to return.     Generally, a permit is not required for standard hydrojet plumbing services that involve clearing existing drain or sewer lines within a private property in Killeen.     The duration of hydrojet plumbing depends on the complexity of the clog and the length of the pipe being cleaned.     For most homes in Killeen, hydrojetting every 1-3 years can be beneficial as preventative maintenance, especially if you have an older home, many mature trees, or frequently experience slow drains.
