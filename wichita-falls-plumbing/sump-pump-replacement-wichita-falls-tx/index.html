    Submersible Sump Pump Replacement in Wichita Falls

    Submersible sump pumps are popular in Wichita Falls for their quiet operation and ability to be fully submerged in the sump pit. When replacing a submersible pump, we carefully remove the old unit, clean the pit, and install a new, appropriately sized pump. We ensure the check valve and discharge piping are correctly connected to prevent backflow and maximize efficiency, crucial for handling heavy Texas rainfalls.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Replacement in Wichita Falls, TX

    Pedestal sump pumps have their motor mounted above the sump pit, making them easier to access for maintenance and often more durable. We provide expert pedestal sump pump replacement in Wichita Falls, TX, ensuring proper alignment and secure mounting. This type is often preferred in shallower pits or where noise isn't a primary concern, offering a robust solution for flood prevention.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Sump Pump Replacement in Wichita Falls

    The cost of sump pump replacement in Wichita Falls, TX typically ranges from $500 to $1,500, depending on the type of pump (submersible vs. pedestal), its horsepower, and any necessary pit modifications.     While it might seem straightforward, professional sump pump replacement is highly recommended. It involves electrical connections, proper plumbing for the discharge line, and ensuring the pump is correctly sized and installed to prevent future flooding and comply with local Wichita Falls codes.

    Sump pumps generally have a lifespan of 7 to 10 years. Even if they appear to be working, their efficiency can decrease over time. Regular inspection and testing are crucial, and replacement is advisable as they approach the end of their expected service life.

    Most standard sump pump replacements can be completed within 2 to 4 hours. More complex installations, such as those requiring new pit excavation or extensive discharge line work, might take longer, up to a full day.

    We install a variety of trusted sump pump brands known for their reliability and performance, including Zoeller, Wayne, Liberty Pumps, and Little Giant. Our plumbers can help you choose the best brand and model to suit your home's specific requirements and budget.

    In many areas of Texas, including Wichita Falls, minor like-for-like sump pump replacements typically do not require a permit.
