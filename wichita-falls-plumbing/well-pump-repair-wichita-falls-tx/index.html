    Submersible Well Pump Repair in Wichita Falls

    Submersible pumps are common in Texas wells, designed to operate submerged within the well casing. When these pumps fail, symptoms often include no water, low water pressure, or strange noises from the well. Our technicians are skilled at diagnosing issues like motor burnout, damaged impellers, or faulty check valves that can affect submersible pump performance. We use specialized equipment for safe and efficient pump extraction and reinstallation, ensuring the integrity of your well system.

    Jet Pump System Repair in Wichita Falls, TX

    Jet pumps, typically found above ground, use an ejector assembly to draw water from the well. Common problems include air leaks in the suction line, clogged nozzles, or a failing motor. These issues can lead to intermittent water flow or a pump that runs continuously without building pressure. DFJ Plumbing can effectively troubleshoot and repair these components, ensuring your jet pump system in Wichita Falls operates at peak efficiency. We also check for proper priming, which is crucial for jet pump function.

    Well Pump Pressure Tank Issues in Wichita Falls

    The pressure tank works in conjunction with your well pump to maintain consistent water pressure and reduce pump cycling. A waterlogged tank, a ruptured diaphragm, or a faulty air valve can cause rapid pump cycling, fluctuating water pressure, or even no water at all. Our team accurately diagnoses pressure tank problems and performs necessary repairs or replacements. We ensure your tank is properly sized and charged for optimal system performance in Wichita Falls.

    Well Pump Electrical Troubleshooting in Wichita Falls, TX

    Many well pump issues stem from electrical problems, including tripped circuit breakers, faulty wiring, or a malfunctioning control box. These can prevent the pump from starting or cause it to run erratically. Our licensed plumbers are also adept at electrical diagnostics, safely testing circuits, relays, and capacitors to identify and repair electrical faults. We ensure all wiring and connections meet Texas electrical codes, providing a safe and reliable power supply to your well pump.
