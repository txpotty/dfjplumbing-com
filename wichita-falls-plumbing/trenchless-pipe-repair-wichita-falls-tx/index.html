    Reliable Trenchless Pipe Repair Services in Wichita Falls, TX

    Cured-in-Place Pipe (CIPP) Lining in Wichita Falls

    CIPP lining is a popular trenchless method where we insert a resin-saturated felt tube into the existing damaged pipe. The liner is then cured in place using heat or air, creating a new, seamless pipe within the old one. This method is excellent for repairing cracks, breaks, and root intrusion without digging up your yard in Wichita Falls.

    Pipe Bursting Services in Wichita Falls, TX

    For severely damaged or collapsed pipes, pipe bursting offers a full replacement solution. A new pipe is pulled through the old one, simultaneously breaking apart the old pipe. This provides a completely new, durable pipe system, often using HDPE (high-density polyethylene) pipe, which is resistant to corrosion and leaks. It's an effective long-term fix for widespread issues in Wichita Falls, TX.

    Sectional Point Repair in Wichita Falls

    When damage is confined to a specific section of pipe, sectional point repair is an ideal trenchless solution. We target only the compromised area, installing a patch or liner to seal the leak or reinforce the pipe. This method is cost-effective and minimizes the scope of work, perfect for isolated issues in Wichita Falls without affecting the entire line.
