    Trenchless Sewer Line Repair in Wichita Falls, TX

    For many sewer line issues, especially those caused by tree roots or pipe corrosion, trenchless repair offers a less invasive solution.     Sewer Pipe Relining in Wichita Falls, TX

    Sewer pipe relining is a trenchless repair technique where a new, durable pipe is created within the existing damaged one. A flexible, epoxy-impregnated liner is inserted into the old pipe and inflated, curing in place to form a seamless, strong, and corrosion-resistant new pipe. This method is effective for addressing cracks, minor breaks, and root intrusion without extensive digging. It extends the life of your sewer system and is an excellent option for preserving landscapes in Wichita Falls.
