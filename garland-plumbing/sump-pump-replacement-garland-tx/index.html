    Submersible Sump Pump Replacement in Garland

    Submersible sump pumps are popular in Garland for their quiet operation and ability to be fully submerged in the sump pit. When replacing a submersible pump, we carefully remove the old unit, clean the pit, and install a new, appropriately sized pump. We ensure the check valve and discharge piping are correctly connected to prevent backflow and maximize efficiency, crucial for handling heavy Texas rainfalls.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Replacement in Garland, TX

    Pedestal sump pumps have their motor mounted above the sump pit, making them easier to access for maintenance and often more durable. We provide expert pedestal sump pump replacement in Garland, TX, ensuring proper alignment and secure mounting. This type is often preferred in shallower pits or where noise isn't a primary concern, offering a robust solution for flood prevention.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Sump Pump Replacement in Garland

    The cost of sump pump replacement in Garland, TX typically ranges from $500 to $1,500, depending on the type of pump (submersible vs. pedestal), its horsepower, and any necessary pit modifications. Battery backup systems will add to this cost, usually between $300 and $800. We provide a detailed, upfront quote after assessing your specific needs.

    A battery backup sump pump system is a secondary pump powered by a battery, designed to activate automatically if your primary sump pump loses power during an outage or fails. This is critical for maintaining flood protection during electrical disruptions caused by severe Texas weather.

    DFJ Plumbing provides a comprehensive warranty on both the sump pump unit itself (as per manufacturer's warranty) and our workmanship. We stand behind our installations, ensuring your peace of mind with a reliable, long-lasting solution.

    To maintain your new sump pump, regularly check the pit for debris, test the pump monthly by pouring water into the pit, and ensure the discharge pipe is clear and free of obstructions. Annual professional inspection by DFJ Plumbing can also help extend its lifespan.

    Sump pumps generally have a lifespan of 7 to 10 years. Even if they appear to be working, their efficiency can decrease over time. Regular inspection and testing are crucial, and replacement is advisable as they approach the end of their expected service life.

    While it might seem straightforward, professional sump pump replacement is highly recommended. It involves electrical connections, proper plumbing for the discharge line, and ensuring the pump is correctly sized and installed to prevent future flooding and comply with local Garland codes.

    Key signs include the pump not turning on, making unusual noises (grinding, rattling), running continuously, visible rust or corrosion, or if it's over 7-10 years old. Any of these could indicate imminent failure and a need for replacement.

    We install a variety of trusted sump pump brands known for their reliability and performance, including Zoeller, Wayne, Liberty Pumps, and Little Giant. Our plumbers can help you choose the best brand and model to suit your home's specific requirements and budget.
