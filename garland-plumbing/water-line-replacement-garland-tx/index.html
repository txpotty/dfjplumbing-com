    Main Water Line Replacement in Garland

    The main water line is the primary pipe delivering water from the municipal supply to your home. When this critical line fails due to age, shifting soil, or severe leaks, a full replacement is often the most cost-effective long-term solution. We expertly excavate, remove the old line, and install new, durable piping, ensuring proper connection to your home's plumbing system and the city's main.

    Trenchless Water Line Replacement in Garland, TX

    For homeowners seeking to minimize disruption to their landscaping, trenchless water line replacement offers an excellent alternative. Using pipe bursting or pipe lining techniques, we can install a new water line with minimal excavation, typically requiring only two small access points. This method is ideal for properties in Garland with mature trees, intricate landscaping, or concrete driveways, saving time and restoration costs.

    Copper Water Line Installation in Garland

    Copper has long been a preferred material for water lines due to its durability, corrosion resistance, and longevity. DFJ Plumbing specializes in installing new copper water lines, known for their ability to withstand high water pressure and resist bacterial growth.     PEX Water Line Installation in Garland, TX

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) piping is a modern, flexible, and increasingly popular choice for water line replacement. It resists corrosion, scaling, and chlorine, making it highly durable for Texas water conditions. PEX is also freeze-resistant, expanding and contracting without bursting, which is a significant advantage during occasional cold snaps in Garland. Its flexibility allows for fewer fittings and faster installation.
