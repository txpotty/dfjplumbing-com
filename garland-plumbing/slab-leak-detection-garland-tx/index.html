    Electronic Acoustic Leak Detection in Garland

    Our team uses advanced electronic listening devices to pinpoint the exact location of a slab leak. These highly sensitive microphones can detect the faint sound of water escaping from a pressurized pipe under your foundation.     Thermal Imaging for Slab Leaks in Garland, TX

    Thermal imaging cameras allow us to visualize temperature differences on surfaces, which can indicate a hidden water leak. As water escapes a hot water line, it creates a distinct thermal signature on the concrete slab. This technique is particularly useful for hot water line leaks and helps us narrow down the search area significantly without any physical disruption to your property in Garland.

    Pressure Testing & Isolation in Garland

    When a leak is suspected, we can perform pressure tests on individual water lines. By isolating sections of your plumbing system and monitoring pressure gauges, we can confirm the presence of a leak and often determine which specific line is compromised. This systematic approach is crucial for homes with complex plumbing layouts, ensuring an accurate diagnosis before any excavation in Garland, TX.

    Video Pipe Inspection for Sewer Leaks in Garland, TX

    While less common for slab leaks, sewer line issues under a slab can also mimic slab leak symptoms. We use high-resolution video cameras to inspect the interior of your drain lines, identifying cracks, blockages, or separations. This is vital if the issue isn't a pressurized water line, helping us determine if a sewer line repair is needed instead for your Garland home.

    Frequently Asked Questions About Slab Leak Detection in Garland

    The cost for professional slab leak detection in Garland, TX typically ranges from $250 to $700, depending on the complexity of the leak and the time required for diagnosis. This initial detection fee is separate from any repair costs, which can vary widely based on the leak's location and required pipe repair.

    In Texas, common pipe materials prone to slab leaks include older copper pipes susceptible to corrosion from hard water or soil acidity, and sometimes PEX or CPVC if they were improperly installed or exposed to extreme temperatures. Galvanized steel pipes, though less common under slabs today, can also corrode and leak.

    A water line slab leak involves pressurized fresh water escaping from supply pipes, often resulting in higher water bills, warm spots, or the sound of running water. A sewer line slab leak involves wastewater leaking from drain pipes, which typically causes foul odors, slow drains, or visible sewage seepage, and is not usually pressurized.

    Many homeowner insurance policies cover the cost of water damage from a sudden or accidental slab leak, and sometimes the cost of accessing the leak for repair. However, coverage varies greatly, so it's essential to check your specific policy with your insurance provider in Garland, TX.

    We stand by the accuracy of our slab leak detection services. While detection itself is a diagnostic service, we ensure our findings are precise. Any subsequent repairs performed by DFJ Plumbing come with our standard labor and parts warranties, which will be clearly outlined for you.

    While the detection process itself typically doesn't require a permit, any subsequent repair work involving the excavation of the slab or significant plumbing alterations will likely require a plumbing permit from the Garland building department. DFJ Plumbing handles all necessary permits for repair work.

    Common signs of a slab leak in Texas homes include unexplained increases in water bills, warm spots on your floor, the sound of running water when no fixtures are in use, damp or mildewy smells, and visible cracks in your foundation or walls. Pooling water outside your home near the foundation can also be a strong indicator.
