    Common signs include recurring clogs in multiple fixtures, slow drains that don't respond to plungers, foul sewage odors, gurgling sounds from drains, or sudden increases in your water bill.     For most homes, a drain camera inspection every 5-7 years is a good preventative measure, especially for older properties or if you have mature trees nearby. If you experience frequent clogs or are planning major renovations, annual or biennial inspections might be beneficial to catch problems early.

    Most residential drain camera inspections take between 30 minutes to 1 hour, depending on the length and complexity of the pipe system. More extensive sewer line inspections or those involving multiple access points might take slightly longer, up to 2 hours.
