    Most standard water heater installations, especially for replacing a similar tank unit, can be completed within 3 to 4 hours.     Common signs that you might need a new water heater include a lack of hot water, rusty or discolored water coming from the hot tap, banging or rumbling noises from the tank, visible leaks around the base of the unit, and an age of 10-15 years or more. If you notice any of these issues, it is best to have a professional inspection.

    The correct size water heater depends on the number of people in your household and your peak hot water demand. Our plumbers will assess your needs to recommend the ideal size.
