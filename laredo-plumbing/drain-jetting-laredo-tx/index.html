    For most homes, preventative drain jetting every 1-2 years is sufficient, especially if you experience frequent minor clogs.     Yes, drain jetting is highly effective at removing tree roots that have infiltrated sewer lines. We use specialized root-cutting nozzles that pulverize the roots with high-pressure water, flushing them out of the system.     Drain snaking typically involves pushing an auger through a clog to create a small opening for water to pass. Drain jetting, or hydro jetting, uses high-pressure water to thoroughly scour the entire pipe wall, removing all buildup and leaving the pipe much cleaner. Jetting is more effective for stubborn blockages and preventative maintenance.

    Generally, a permit is not required for routine drain jetting services as it is a maintenance activity within existing plumbing.     While drain jetting focuses on clearing existing pipes, we work with various reliable brands for replacement parts if needed, such as Charlotte Pipe for PVC, Cast Iron Soil Pipe Institute (CISPI) standards for cast iron, and various manufacturers for cleanout assemblies.
