    Primary Sump Pump Installation in Waco

    A primary sump pump is your main defense against basement flooding. We install robust, electrically powered pumps designed to automatically activate when water levels rise in the sump pit.     Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Waco, TX

    Power outages during heavy storms are a real concern in Texas, which is why a battery backup sump pump is a smart addition. This system automatically takes over if your primary pump loses power, ensuring continuous flood protection. We install reliable backup units that can operate for hours, giving you peace of mind even when the electricity goes out in Waco.

    Combination Sump Pump Systems in Waco

    For ultimate protection, consider a combination sump pump system which integrates both a primary and a battery backup pump into one unit. These systems are pre-assembled for easier installation and offer a compact solution for flood prevention. DFJ Plumbing can install these high-efficiency units, ensuring your Waco home has dual layers of defense against water damage.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Waco, TX

    Pedestal sump pumps have their motor mounted above the sump pit, making them easier to access for maintenance and repair.
