    Frequently Asked Questions About Gas Water Heater Repair in Waco

    The cost of gas water heater repair in Waco, TX can vary widely depending on the issue. Minor repairs like a thermocouple replacement might range from $150-$350, while more complex issues such as a gas valve replacement could be $400-$800 or more. We always provide a detailed, upfront estimate after diagnosis.

    DFJ Plumbing stands behind our work with a comprehensive warranty on parts and labor for all gas water heater repairs. Specific warranty details will be provided at the time of service, giving you peace of mind for your investment in your Waco, TX home.

    Look out for a lack of hot water, inconsistent water temperature, a pilot light that won't stay lit, unusual noises like rumbling or popping, a rotten egg smell (indicating a gas leak), or visible leaks around the tank. Any of these warrant a professional inspection.

    Due to the inherent risks associated with natural gas and potential carbon monoxide poisoning, gas water heater repairs should always be handled by a licensed professional. DIY attempts can be dangerous and may void warranties or violate local Waco, TX plumbing codes.

    Generally, minor repairs like replacing a thermocouple or a thermostat do not require a permit. However, if the repair involves significant alterations to the gas line or venting system, or if the unit needs to be replaced, a permit from the Waco building department might be necessary. We handle all necessary permitting.

    A thermocouple is a safety device in a gas water heater that senses if the pilot light is lit. If it doesn't detect heat, it shuts off the gas supply to prevent gas leaks. A faulty thermocouple is a very common reason for a pilot light not staying lit and is a critical safety component.

    Most common gas water heater repairs can be completed within 1 to 3 hours. However, more complex issues or if special parts need to be ordered could extend the timeline. We strive for efficiency without compromising safety or quality.

    If you smell gas, immediately evacuate your home, do not turn on or off any lights or appliances, and call your gas company's emergency line and then DFJ Plumbing from a safe location. This indicates a potentially dangerous gas leak that requires immediate professional attention.
