    Standard Hot Water Tank Tune-Up in Longview

    DFJ Plumbing offers a comprehensive tune-up for conventional hot water tanks, focusing on critical components. This includes draining the tank to remove sediment buildup, which is crucial in areas like Longview with hard water, and inspecting the anode rod for signs of corrosion. We also check heating elements, thermostat settings, and all pipe connections for leaks. Our goal is to optimize your tank's performance and prevent future issues.

    Anode Rod Inspection & Replacement in Longview, TX

    The anode rod is your hot water tank's sacrificial protector against corrosion, and it's especially important with the mineral content often found in Texas water. During our maintenance, we pull and inspect the anode rod. If it's significantly depleted, we recommend and perform a replacement to extend the life of your tank in Longview, TX, preventing rust from eating away at the tank lining.

    Pressure Relief Valve Testing in Longview

    A properly functioning temperature and pressure relief (T&P) valve is a crucial safety device for any hot water tank. Our technicians will test this valve to ensure it's not stuck or leaking, preventing dangerous pressure buildup within the tank. We verify it opens and closes correctly, and if it fails, we will suggest immediate replacement to keep your Longview home safe and compliant with plumbing codes.

    Sediment Flushing & Cleaning in Longview, TX

    Over time, minerals and sediment settle at the bottom of hot water tanks, reducing efficiency and potentially leading to premature failure. Our maintenance includes a thorough flushing of the tank to remove these deposits. This process helps your tank heat water more efficiently and quietly, a vital step for any hot water tank in Longview, TX, where hard water is a common concern.

    Bradford White & Rheem Tank Maintenance in Longview
