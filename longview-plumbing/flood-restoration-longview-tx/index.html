    The duration of flood restoration varies based on the severity of the damage. Initial water extraction might take a few hours, but the drying process often requires 3 to 5 days, depending on humidity and the amount of moisture. Complete restoration, including repairs, can extend to several weeks or more for severe cases.

    We use state-of-the-art equipment for efficient flood restoration, including high-capacity submersible pumps, industrial-grade wet vacuums, powerful air movers from brands like Dri-Eaz and Phoenix, and commercial dehumidifiers.     Signs of hidden moisture after a flood include musty odors, peeling paint or wallpaper, warped flooring, discolored drywall, and visible mold growth in unexpected places. Our technicians use moisture meters and thermal imaging cameras to detect moisture that isn't visible to the naked eye.
