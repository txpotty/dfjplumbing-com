    The cost for copper pipe leak repair in Midland varies widely, typically ranging from $200 for a minor, accessible pinhole leak to over $2,000 for complex repairs like under-slab leaks requiring extensive work.     We work with all standard types and brands of copper piping, including Type K, L, and M, which are commonly found in residential and commercial plumbing systems. Our technicians are proficient in repairing and integrating piping from manufacturers like Mueller Industries, Wieland, and Cerro, ensuring compatibility and quality.

    Yes, DFJ Plumbing stands behind the quality of our work. We offer a comprehensive warranty on all our copper pipe leak repairs, covering both parts and labor.     Copper pipe leaks are often caused by corrosion, especially from hard or acidic water common in parts of Texas. Other causes include excessive water pressure, improper installation, shifting foundations putting stress on joints, and occasional freezing temperatures that can cause pipes to burst. Pinhole leaks are a common symptom of internal corrosion.

    Type K, L, and M copper pipes differ primarily in their wall thickness and thus their pressure ratings. Type K has the thickest walls and is used for underground service and main water lines. Type L is common for interior water supply and radiant heating. Type M has the thinnest walls and is typically used for residential drainage and less critical applications. We are skilled in repairing all types.

    To prevent future copper pipe leaks, consider installing a whole-house water softener if you have hard water, as mineral buildup can lead to corrosion. Maintaining appropriate water pressure, ensuring proper pipe insulation during cold weather, and scheduling periodic plumbing inspections can also significantly extend the life of your copper pipes.
