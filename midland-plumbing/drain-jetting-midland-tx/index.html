    Drain snaking typically involves pushing an auger through a clog to create a small opening for water to pass. Jetting is more effective for stubborn blockages and preventative maintenance.

    A typical residential drain jetting service can take anywhere from 1 to 3 hours, depending on the complexity and location of the clog. More severe blockages or main sewer line jetting may require additional time for preparation and thorough cleaning.

    We strongly advise against using a home pressure washer for drain jetting. These machines lack the necessary power, specialized nozzles, and safety features of professional equipment.
