    Primary Sump Pump Installation in Midland

    A primary sump pump is your main defense against basement flooding. We install robust, electrically powered pumps designed to automatically activate when water levels rise in the sump pit.     Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Midland, TX

    Power outages during heavy storms are a real concern in Texas, which is why a battery backup sump pump is a smart addition. This system automatically takes over if your primary pump loses power, ensuring continuous flood protection. We install reliable backup units that can operate for hours, giving you peace of mind even when the electricity goes out in Midland.

    Combination Sump Pump Systems in Midland

    For ultimate protection, consider a combination sump pump system which integrates both a primary and a battery backup pump into one unit. These systems are pre-assembled for easier installation and offer a compact solution for flood prevention. DFJ Plumbing can install these high-efficiency units, ensuring your Midland home has dual layers of defense against water damage.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Midland, TX

    Pedestal sump pumps have their motor mounted above the sump pit, making them easier to access for maintenance and repair. Our team is skilled in installing these types of pumps, ensuring proper pit depth and discharge line setup for optimal performance in Midland, TX.

    Submersible Sump Pump Installation in Midland

    Submersible sump pumps sit directly inside the sump pit, operating quietly and taking up less space. They are typically more powerful and durable, making them a popular choice for many homeowners.
