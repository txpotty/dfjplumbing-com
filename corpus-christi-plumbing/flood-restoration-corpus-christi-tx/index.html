    Our mold prevention strategy involves rapid water extraction, thorough drying with commercial-grade equipment, and applying EPA-approved antimicrobial agents to affected surfaces.     We use state-of-the-art equipment for efficient flood restoration, including high-capacity submersible pumps, industrial-grade wet vacuums, powerful air movers from brands like Dri-Eaz and Phoenix, and commercial dehumidifiers.
