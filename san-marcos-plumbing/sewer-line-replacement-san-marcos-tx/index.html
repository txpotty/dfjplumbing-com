    Trenchless Sewer Line Repair & Replacement in San Marcos, TX

    Trenchless technology, such as pipe bursting or pipe lining, offers a less invasive alternative to traditional methods. Pipe bursting involves pulling a new pipe through the old one, fracturing the old pipe in place. Pipe lining inserts a new, durable epoxy-coated liner into the existing pipe. Both methods significantly reduce the need for extensive excavation, preserving your yard and landscaping in San Marcos, TX while providing a long-lasting solution.
