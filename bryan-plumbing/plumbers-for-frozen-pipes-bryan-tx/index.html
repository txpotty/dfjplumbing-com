    Texas plumbing codes, while generally less stringent on freeze protection than northern states, do require proper insulation for pipes in unconditioned spaces and minimum burial depths for water lines to reduce freezing risk.     Common signs of a frozen pipe include a sudden drop in water pressure, no water coming from a specific faucet, visible frost on an exposed pipe, or strange gurgling sounds from your drains.     You can attempt to thaw an exposed, easily accessible pipe with a hairdryer on a low setting or warm towels.     To prevent future freezing in Bryan, insulate exposed pipes, especially those in attics, crawl spaces, and exterior walls. Disconnect garden hoses and drain outdoor spigots.
