    Direct Slab Leak Repair in Mission, TX

    Slab Leak Pipe Rerouting in Mission

    For more complex or recurring slab leaks, or when the original piping is severely deteriorated, pipe rerouting is an excellent long-term solution. This method involves abandoning the leaky pipe under the slab and installing new plumbing lines through walls or the attic, bypassing the foundation entirely. This eliminates future slab leak risks from that particular line and is often preferred for older homes in Mission with aging copper or galvanized pipes.

    PEX Repiping for Slab Leaks in Mission, TX

    If multiple slab leaks indicate a systemic issue with your home's original plumbing, a full PEX repipe might be the most cost-effective and durable solution. PEX tubing is flexible, corrosion-resistant, and less prone to leaks caused by foundation shifts, making it ideal for Texas homes. We can replace all the water supply lines in your Mission home, providing a brand-new, leak-free plumbing system that will last for decades.
