    While some minor clogs or leaky faucets can be DIY projects, most residential plumbing repairs are best left to professionals. Incorrectly performed repairs can lead to more significant damage, costly leaks, or even safety hazards, especially with gas lines or water heaters. For durable, code-compliant solutions in Mission, always call a licensed plumber.

    A slab leak is a leak in the water pipes located beneath your home's concrete foundation. They are common in Texas due to shifting clay soils, which can put stress on pipes, and the prevalence of homes built on slab foundations. Signs include warm spots on the floor, sudden increases in water bills, or the sound of running water when no fixtures are in use.

    To prevent burst pipes during a Texas freeze, insulate exposed pipes, especially those in unheated areas like attics or crawl spaces. Disconnect garden hoses, open cabinet doors under sinks to allow warm air in, and consider letting faucets drip slowly. If you're leaving your Mission home, consider shutting off the main water supply and draining your pipes.

    Common signs that you need professional drain cleaning include slow drains, gurgling sounds from your pipes, foul odors coming from drains, and water backing up in sinks or showers. If you're experiencing any of these issues in your Mission home, it's time to call a professional before the clog worsens.

    Hard water contains high levels of dissolved minerals, primarily calcium and magnesium, common in Mission's water supply. Soft water has these minerals removed. Hard water can cause mineral buildup in pipes, reduced soap lather, and appliance damage, while soft water is gentler on plumbing and skin.
