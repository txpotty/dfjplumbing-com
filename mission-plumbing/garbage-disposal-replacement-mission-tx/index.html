    Frequently Asked Questions About Garbage Disposal Replacement in Mission

    The cost for garbage disposal replacement in Mission, TX typically ranges from $200 to $600. This includes the new unit itself, which can vary from $100 for a basic 1/3 HP model to over $300 for a high-end 1 HP unit, plus labor costs. Factors like the disposal's horsepower, brand, and any necessary plumbing modifications can influence the final price.

    A continuous feed disposal operates when you flip a wall switch, allowing you to add food waste while it's running. A batch feed disposal only operates when a special stopper is inserted into the drain, making it generally safer as it prevents accidental activation while hands are near the opening.

    To prolong the life of your new disposal, avoid putting fibrous foods like celery, corn husks, or onion skins down it. Also, do not dispose of grease, coffee grounds, or non-food items. Always run cold water before, during, and after use, and grind small amounts of citrus peels or ice cubes periodically to clean the unit.

    Yes, new garbage disposals typically come with a manufacturer's warranty, which varies by brand and model, usually ranging from 1 to 10 years. DFJ Plumbing also stands behind our workmanship with our own service guarantee, ensuring your installation is done right the first time.

    Generally, a direct replacement of a garbage disposal with a similar unit does not require a plumbing permit in Mission, TX.     Most garbage disposal replacements can be completed within 1 to 2 hours. This timeframe includes disconnecting the old unit, installing the new one, and testing it thoroughly. More complex situations, such as significant plumbing modifications or electrical work, might extend the duration slightly.

    Key signs you need a new garbage disposal include persistent leaks from the unit's housing, a humming noise without grinding, frequent clogs despite proper usage, foul odors that won't go away, or if the unit simply won't turn on.
