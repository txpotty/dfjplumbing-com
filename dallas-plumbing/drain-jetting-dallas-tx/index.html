    Yes, drain jetting is highly effective at removing tree roots that have infiltrated sewer lines.     We strongly advise against using a home pressure washer for drain jetting. These machines lack the necessary power, specialized nozzles, and safety features of professional equipment.     While drain jetting focuses on clearing existing pipes, we work with various reliable brands for replacement parts if needed, such as Charlotte Pipe for PVC, Cast Iron Soil Pipe Institute (CISPI) standards for cast iron, and various manufacturers for cleanout assemblies.
