    Signs of a failing expansion tank include water dripping from the tank's air valve, rust or corrosion on the tank's exterior, or your water heater frequently cycling on and off. This constant pressure can prematurely wear out water heater components, relief valves, and even cause leaks or ruptures. The expansion tank absorbs this excess volume, protecting your entire system.
