    Pipe Bursting Sewer Line Replacement in Little Elm

    Pipe bursting is a trenchless method used for full sewer line replacement. It involves pulling a new pipe through the old one, simultaneously breaking apart the existing pipe. This creates a seamless, durable replacement, often using high-density polyethylene (HDPE) pipe, which is highly resistant to corrosion and root intrusion, common problems in Little Elm's older homes. This method is ideal when the existing pipe is severely damaged or collapsed.

    Cured-In-Place Pipe (CIPP) Relining in Little Elm, TX

    CIPP relining creates a new pipe within an existing damaged sewer line. A resin-saturated felt tube is inserted into the old pipe and then inflated, curing in place to form a smooth, durable, and watertight liner. This technique is excellent for repairing cracks, leaks, and minor offsets without needing to dig up the entire line. It’s a fast and effective solution for many common sewer problems faced by Texas homeowners.
