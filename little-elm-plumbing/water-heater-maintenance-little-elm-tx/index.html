    DFJ Plumbing stands behind all our services with a robust workmanship warranty. We ensure that our maintenance is performed correctly and effectively. Specific warranty details will be provided upon service completion.

    Most manufacturers and plumbing experts recommend annual maintenance for your water heater. In areas like Little Elm, TX with harder water, more frequent flushing every 6 to 12 months can be beneficial to prevent sediment buildup.

    The anode rod is a metal rod inside your water heater that sacrifices itself to prevent the steel tank from corroding. It attracts corrosive elements in the water. Regular inspection and replacement of a depleted anode rod are crucial for extending the lifespan of your water heater, especially with varying water quality in Texas.

    While some basic tasks like flushing a tank might seem DIY-friendly, professional maintenance is highly recommended. Our technicians have the right tools, knowledge, and experience to perform thorough safety checks, identify potential hazards, and ensure compliance with local codes, which a DIY approach might miss.

    Common signs include strange noises like rumbling or popping, rusty or discolored hot water, a rotten egg smell, reduced hot water supply, or a noticeable drop in water pressure. If you observe any of these, it's time for a professional check.
