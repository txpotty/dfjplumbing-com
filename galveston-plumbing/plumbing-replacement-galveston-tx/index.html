    If you have isolated leaks on newer pipes, a repair might suffice. Our plumbers can conduct an inspection, often with camera technology for sewer lines, to provide an accurate assessment and recommendation.

    For water heater replacements, we primarily recommend and install reliable brands such as Rheem, AO Smith, Bradford White, and Navien. These manufacturers are known for their durability, energy efficiency, and excellent warranty coverage, providing long-term value for your Galveston home.

    While some minor plumbing repairs can be DIY, major replacements like water heaters, sewer lines, or repiping should always be handled by licensed professionals. These jobs involve complex systems, potential safety hazards (gas, high water pressure), and strict code compliance. Improper installation can lead to leaks, damage, and even severe safety risks, costing more in the long run.

    Key signs include frequent, recurring leaks in the same area, discolored water, very low water pressure throughout the house, extensive corrosion visible on pipes, or a major system component like a water heater being over 10-15 years old and failing. If repair costs approach the cost of replacement, replacement is often the better long-term investment.

    The timeline for plumbing replacement depends on the complexity of the job. A water heater replacement usually takes 2-4 hours. A main water line or sewer line replacement can take 1-3 days. Whole-home repiping projects typically span 3-7 days, depending on the size of the house and accessibility.

    Replacing old pipes with PEX or copper offers numerous benefits. PEX is flexible, corrosion-resistant, and less prone to bursting in freezes, while copper is durable and long-lasting. Both improve water quality, eliminate leaks from old pipes, and significantly boost water pressure and flow throughout your Galveston home.
