    Traditional Trench Sewer Line Replacement in Galveston

    For significant damage or specific pipe configurations, traditional trenching may be the most effective method.     Trenchless Sewer Line Repair & Replacement in Galveston, TX

    Trenchless technology, such as pipe bursting or pipe lining, offers a less invasive alternative to traditional methods. Pipe bursting involves pulling a new pipe through the old one, fracturing the old pipe in place. Pipe lining inserts a new, durable epoxy-coated liner into the existing pipe. Both methods significantly reduce the need for extensive excavation, preserving your yard and landscaping in Galveston, TX while providing a long-lasting solution.
