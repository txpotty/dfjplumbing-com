    Leaking Grease Trap Repair in Galveston, TX

    A leaking grease trap can cause significant property damage, foul odors, and potential health code violations. Leaks often stem from corroded tanks, cracked seams, or failing pipe connections.     Frequently Asked Questions About Grease Trap Repair in Galveston

    The cost of grease trap repair in Galveston, TX can vary widely, typically ranging from $300 for minor clogs or baffle repairs to over $2,000 for extensive damage, tank replacement, or emergency services requiring hydro-jetting.     Grease traps are smaller, typically located inside or near the kitchen, and are suitable for low-volume establishments. Grease interceptors are much larger, often installed underground outside the building, and are designed to handle the high FOG volumes from large restaurants and commercial facilities. Both serve the same purpose of preventing FOGs from entering the sewer system.

    For minor repairs like clearing a clog or replacing a baffle, permits are typically not required. However, if the repair involves significant alteration to the plumbing system, relocating the trap, or replacing the entire unit, a plumbing permit from the Galveston permitting office may be necessary. We can advise you on permit requirements during our assessment.

    Key signs include slow drainage in your kitchen sinks, foul odors emanating from drains or the trap itself, visible FOGs in the wastewater leaving the trap, or an overflow. If you notice any of these, it's time to call a professional for an inspection to prevent further damage or violations.

    A broken grease trap can release large amounts of FOGs into the municipal sewer system, leading to blockages and sanitary sewer overflows. These overflows can contaminate local waterways, harm aquatic life, and spread bacteria, posing significant environmental and public health risks in Galveston, TX. Proper repair and maintenance are crucial for environmental protection.

    Most standard grease trap repairs, such as clearing a blockage or replacing a baffle, can be completed within a few hours. More complex issues, like repairing a leaking tank or extensive pipe damage, may take a full day or require multiple visits. We always work to minimize disruption to your business in Galveston.

    While minor clogs might seem DIY-fixable, grease trap repair is generally best left to licensed plumbers. These systems involve complex plumbing, potential health hazards from wastewater, and strict local codes in Texas. Improper repairs can lead to greater damage, environmental fines, or health code violations for your business.
