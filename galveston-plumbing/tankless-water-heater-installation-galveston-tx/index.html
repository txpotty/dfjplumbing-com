    To maintain efficiency and extend the lifespan of your tankless unit, annual flushing to remove mineral buildup is recommended, especially in areas like Galveston with hard water.     A standard tankless water heater installation, replacing an existing tank, typically takes 4 to 8 hours. If extensive plumbing or gas line modifications are required, it may take a full day. Our team works efficiently to restore your hot water as quickly as possible.

    While this page focuses on tankless, signs you might need a new water heater, tankless or otherwise, include a lack of hot water, rust-colored water, strange noises from the unit, or frequent leaks. For tankless systems specifically, frequent error codes or a significant drop in hot water flow could indicate a need for service or replacement.

    Sizing a tankless water heater depends on your household's peak hot water demand, measured in gallons per minute (GPM), and the temperature rise needed. Our plumbers will assess the number of hot water fixtures in your home and your usage patterns to recommend the appropriate GPM and BTU rating for your specific needs in Galveston.
