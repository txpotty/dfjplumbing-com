    Frequently Asked Questions About Garbage Disposal Replacement in Galveston

    The cost for garbage disposal replacement in Galveston, TX typically ranges from $200 to $600. This includes the new unit itself, which can vary from $100 for a basic 1/3 HP model to over $300 for a high-end 1 HP unit, plus labor costs. Factors like the disposal's horsepower, brand, and any necessary plumbing modifications can influence the final price.

    Yes, new garbage disposals typically come with a manufacturer's warranty, which varies by brand and model, usually ranging from 1 to 10 years. DFJ Plumbing also stands behind our workmanship with our own service guarantee, ensuring your installation is done right the first time.

    Key signs you need a new garbage disposal include persistent leaks from the unit's housing, a humming noise without grinding, frequent clogs despite proper usage, foul odors that won't go away, or if the unit simply won't turn on. These symptoms often indicate internal motor or component failure.

    For light use in a single-person household, 1/3 HP might suffice. Most homeowners opt for 1/2 HP or 3/4 HP for general use, which handles common food waste well. For large families, frequent cooking, or tougher waste like small bones, a 1 HP unit offers the best performance and durability.

    We replace all major brands of garbage disposals, including InSinkErator, Waste King, Moen, GE, KitchenAid, and Whirlaway. Our plumbers are familiar with the specific installation requirements and features of each brand, ensuring a proper and efficient replacement every time.

    Generally, a direct replacement of a garbage disposal with a similar unit does not require a plumbing permit in Galveston, TX. However, if there are significant modifications to your kitchen plumbing or electrical system involved, a permit might be necessary. Our plumbers are knowledgeable about local codes and will advise you if any permits are needed.

    A continuous feed disposal operates when you flip a wall switch, allowing you to add food waste while it's running. A batch feed disposal only operates when a special stopper is inserted into the drain, making it generally safer as it prevents accidental activation while hands are near the opening.
