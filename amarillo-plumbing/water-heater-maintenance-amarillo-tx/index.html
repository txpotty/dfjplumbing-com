    Standard Tank Water Heater Maintenance in Amarillo

    Our standard tank water heater maintenance includes flushing the tank to remove sediment buildup, which is common with hard water in many parts of Texas. We also inspect the anode rod, test the temperature and pressure relief valve, and check for any leaks or corrosion on the tank and connections. This service helps prevent premature failure and improves heating efficiency, keeping your energy bills down.

    Tankless Water Heater Maintenance in Amarillo, TX

    Tankless water heaters require specialized maintenance, primarily involving a descaling flush to remove mineral deposits that can restrict flow and reduce efficiency. Our technicians in Amarillo, TX use commercial-grade solutions to clean the heat exchanger, ensuring your unit continues to provide endless hot water efficiently. We also inspect the igniter, fan, and exhaust venting for optimal performance and safety.

    Electric Water Heater Tune-Up in Amarillo

    For electric water heaters, our maintenance service focuses on checking heating elements, thermostats, and electrical connections. We ensure proper wiring and test for any short circuits or power fluctuations that could impact performance. Sediment flushing is also performed, just like with gas models, to prevent buildup and protect the lower heating element from burning out prematurely in Amarillo.

    Gas Water Heater Safety Checks in Amarillo, TX

    Gas water heater maintenance involves crucial safety inspections in Amarillo, TX. We check for gas leaks, proper ventilation of exhaust gases, and the functionality of the pilot light or igniter. We also clean the burner assembly and inspect the thermocouple. These checks are vital to prevent carbon monoxide exposure and ensure the safe operation of your gas-fired appliance.

    Water Heater Anode Rod Inspection in Amarillo

    The anode rod is a sacrificial component that protects your water heater tank from corrosion. During maintenance in Amarillo, we inspect the condition of the anode rod and recommend replacement if it's significantly depleted. Replacing a worn anode rod can extend the life of your water heater by several years, preventing costly tank leaks.
