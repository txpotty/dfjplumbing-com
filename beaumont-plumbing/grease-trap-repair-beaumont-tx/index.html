    Leaking Grease Trap Repair in Beaumont, TX

    A leaking grease trap can cause significant property damage, foul odors, and potential health code violations. Leaks often stem from corroded tanks, cracked seams, or failing pipe connections. We use leak detection techniques to pinpoint the source and provide targeted repairs, which may involve patching the tank, sealing connections, or replacing damaged sections of the trap or associated plumbing.     Damaged Baffle Repair in Beaumont

    The baffles inside a grease trap are critical for separating FOGs from wastewater. Our service includes inspecting the condition of all baffles and repairing or replacing them as needed, often using durable, corrosion-resistant materials. Proper baffle function is key to compliance with wastewater regulations in Beaumont.
