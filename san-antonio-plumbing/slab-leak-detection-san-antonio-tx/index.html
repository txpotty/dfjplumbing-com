    Electronic Acoustic Leak Detection in San Antonio

    Our team uses advanced electronic listening devices to pinpoint the exact location of a slab leak. These highly sensitive microphones can detect the faint sound of water escaping from a pressurized pipe under your foundation. This non-invasive method helps us avoid unnecessary concrete breaking, saving you time and money on repairs. It's especially effective for copper and PEX lines commonly found in San Antonio homes.

    Thermal Imaging for Slab Leaks in San Antonio, TX

    Thermal imaging cameras allow us to visualize temperature differences on surfaces, which can indicate a hidden water leak. As water escapes a hot water line, it creates a distinct thermal signature on the concrete slab. This technique is particularly useful for hot water line leaks and helps us narrow down the search area significantly without any physical disruption to your property in San Antonio.

    Pressure Testing & Isolation in San Antonio

    When a leak is suspected, we can perform pressure tests on individual water lines. By isolating sections of your plumbing system and monitoring pressure gauges, we can confirm the presence of a leak and often determine which specific line is compromised. This systematic approach is crucial for homes with complex plumbing layouts, ensuring an accurate diagnosis before any excavation in San Antonio, TX.

    Video Pipe Inspection for Sewer Leaks in San Antonio, TX

    While less common for slab leaks, sewer line issues under a slab can also mimic slab leak symptoms.     A water line slab leak involves pressurized fresh water escaping from supply pipes, often resulting in higher water bills, warm spots, or the sound of running water. A sewer line slab leak involves wastewater leaking from drain pipes, which typically causes foul odors, slow drains, or visible sewage seepage, and is not usually pressurized.

    While you can look for the signs, accurately detecting a slab leak requires specialized equipment and expertise that most homeowners don't possess. DIY attempts often lead to more damage or misdiagnosis, making professional detection a more cost-effective and reliable solution in the long run.

    Common signs of a slab leak in Texas homes include unexplained increases in water bills, warm spots on your floor, the sound of running water when no fixtures are in use, damp or mildewy smells, and visible cracks in your foundation or walls. Pooling water outside your home near the foundation can also be a strong indicator.

    Many homeowner insurance policies cover the cost of water damage from a sudden or accidental slab leak, and sometimes the cost of accessing the leak for repair. However, coverage varies greatly, so it's essential to check your specific policy with your insurance provider in San Antonio, TX.
