    Hard water contains high levels of dissolved minerals, primarily calcium and magnesium, common in San Antonio's water supply. Soft water has these minerals removed. Hard water can cause mineral buildup in pipes, reduced soap lather, and appliance damage, while soft water is gentler on plumbing and skin.

    To prevent burst pipes during a Texas freeze, insulate exposed pipes, especially those in unheated areas like attics or crawl spaces. Disconnect garden hoses, open cabinet doors under sinks to allow warm air in, and consider letting faucets drip slowly. If you're leaving your San Antonio home, consider shutting off the main water supply and draining your pipes.

    Common signs that you need professional drain cleaning include slow drains, gurgling sounds from your pipes, foul odors coming from drains, and water backing up in sinks or showers.     While some minor clogs or leaky faucets can be DIY projects, most residential plumbing repairs are best left to professionals.     A slab leak is a leak in the water pipes located beneath your home's concrete foundation.
