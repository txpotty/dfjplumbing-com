    Frequently Asked Questions About Gas Water Heater Repair in Conroe

    A thermocouple is a safety device in a gas water heater that senses if the pilot light is lit. If it doesn't detect heat, it shuts off the gas supply to prevent gas leaks. A faulty thermocouple is a very common reason for a pilot light not staying lit and is a critical safety component.

    Due to the inherent risks associated with natural gas and potential carbon monoxide poisoning, gas water heater repairs should always be handled by a licensed professional. DIY attempts can be dangerous and may void warranties or violate local Conroe, TX plumbing codes.
