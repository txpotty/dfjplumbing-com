    Installing a gas water heater involves working with natural gas lines, water lines, and exhaust venting, which can be dangerous if not done correctly.     Yes, in Conroe, a permit is generally required for gas water heater installation to ensure compliance with local building and plumbing codes. DFJ Plumbing handles all necessary permit applications and ensures your installation passes inspection.

    Common signs include a lack of hot water, rust-colored water, strange noises like rumbling or popping from the tank, or visible leaks around the base of the unit. Older units, especially those over 10-12 years old, are also candidates for replacement.
