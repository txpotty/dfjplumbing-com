    Electronic Acoustic Leak Detection in Conroe

    Our team uses advanced electronic listening devices to pinpoint the exact location of a slab leak. These highly sensitive microphones can detect the faint sound of water escaping from a pressurized pipe under your foundation.     Thermal Imaging for Slab Leaks in Conroe, TX

    Thermal imaging cameras allow us to visualize temperature differences on surfaces, which can indicate a hidden water leak. As water escapes a hot water line, it creates a distinct thermal signature on the concrete slab. This technique is particularly useful for hot water line leaks and helps us narrow down the search area significantly without any physical disruption to your property in Conroe.

    Pressure Testing & Isolation in Conroe

    When a leak is suspected, we can perform pressure tests on individual water lines. By isolating sections of your plumbing system and monitoring pressure gauges, we can confirm the presence of a leak and often determine which specific line is compromised. This systematic approach is crucial for homes with complex plumbing layouts, ensuring an accurate diagnosis before any excavation in Conroe, TX.

    Video Pipe Inspection for Sewer Leaks in Conroe, TX

    While less common for slab leaks, sewer line issues under a slab can also mimic slab leak symptoms.
