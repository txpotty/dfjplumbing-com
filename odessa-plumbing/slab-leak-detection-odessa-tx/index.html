    Thermal Imaging for Slab Leaks in Odessa, TX

    Thermal imaging cameras allow us to visualize temperature differences on surfaces, which can indicate a hidden water leak.     Common signs of a slab leak in Texas homes include unexplained increases in water bills, warm spots on your floor, the sound of running water when no fixtures are in use, damp or mildewy smells, and visible cracks in your foundation or walls. Pooling water outside your home near the foundation can also be a strong indicator.

    A water line slab leak involves pressurized fresh water escaping from supply pipes, often resulting in higher water bills, warm spots, or the sound of running water. A sewer line slab leak involves wastewater leaking from drain pipes, which typically causes foul odors, slow drains, or visible sewage seepage, and is not usually pressurized.

    Many homeowner insurance policies cover the cost of water damage from a sudden or accidental slab leak, and sometimes the cost of accessing the leak for repair. However, coverage varies greatly, so it's essential to check your specific policy with your insurance provider in Odessa, TX.

    In Texas, common pipe materials prone to slab leaks include older copper pipes susceptible to corrosion from hard water or soil acidity, and sometimes PEX or CPVC if they were improperly installed or exposed to extreme temperatures.
