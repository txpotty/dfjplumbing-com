    Yes, installing a new tankless water heater or replacing an existing one in Georgetown typically requires a plumbing permit. This ensures the installation meets local building and safety codes. DFJ Plumbing handles all necessary permitting paperwork for you, ensuring a compliant installation.

    While it may seem appealing, tankless water heater installation is complex and should only be performed by a licensed professional.
