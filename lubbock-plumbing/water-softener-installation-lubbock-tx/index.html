    Salt-Based Water Softener Systems in Lubbock, TX

    Salt-based water softeners are the most common and effective type, using ion exchange to remove hard minerals like calcium and magnesium. DFJ Plumbing specializes in the installation of these traditional systems, ensuring proper brine tank setup and regeneration cycles. We can recommend efficient models that minimize salt and water consumption for your Lubbock, TX home.

    Salt-Free Water Conditioner Installation in Lubbock

    For homeowners looking for an alternative to traditional softeners, we also offer salt-free water conditioner installation. These systems condition the water to prevent scale buildup without removing beneficial minerals or adding salt. While not true softeners, they are an excellent option for scale prevention and are popular in many Lubbock households.
