    Primary Sump Pump Installation in Lubbock

    A primary sump pump is your main defense against basement flooding. We install robust, electrically powered pumps designed to automatically activate when water levels rise in the sump pit. Our plumbers will assess your basement's water intrusion risks, considering local water tables and soil types common in Lubbock, to recommend the optimal pump size and horsepower for effective water removal.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in Lubbock, TX

    Power outages during heavy storms are a real concern in Texas, which is why a battery backup sump pump is a smart addition. This system automatically takes over if your primary pump loses power, ensuring continuous flood protection. We install reliable backup units that can operate for hours, giving you peace of mind even when the electricity goes out in Lubbock.

    Combination Sump Pump Systems in Lubbock

    For ultimate protection, consider a combination sump pump system which integrates both a primary and a battery backup pump into one unit. These systems are pre-assembled for easier installation and offer a compact solution for flood prevention. DFJ Plumbing can install these high-efficiency units, ensuring your Lubbock home has dual layers of defense against water damage.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in Lubbock, TX

    Pedestal sump pumps have their motor mounted above the sump pit, making them easier to access for maintenance and repair. They are often a good choice for shallow sump pits or areas where space is a concern. Our team is skilled in installing these types of pumps, ensuring proper pit depth and discharge line setup for optimal performance in Lubbock, TX.

    Submersible Sump Pump Installation in Lubbock

    Submersible sump pumps sit directly inside the sump pit, operating quietly and taking up less space. They are typically more powerful and durable, making them a popular choice for many homeowners. We handle the complete installation of submersible units, including proper sealing and electrical connections, tailored to the specific needs of your Lubbock property.
