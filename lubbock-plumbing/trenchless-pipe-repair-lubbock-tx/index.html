    Reliable Trenchless Pipe Repair Services in Lubbock, TX

    Cured-in-Place Pipe (CIPP) Lining in Lubbock

    CIPP lining is a popular trenchless method where we insert a resin-saturated felt tube into the existing damaged pipe. The liner is then cured in place using heat or air, creating a new, seamless pipe within the old one. This method is excellent for repairing cracks, breaks, and root intrusion without digging up your yard in Lubbock.

    Pipe Bursting Services in Lubbock, TX

    For severely damaged or collapsed pipes, pipe bursting offers a full replacement solution. A new pipe is pulled through the old one, simultaneously breaking apart the old pipe. This provides a completely new, durable pipe system, often using HDPE (high-density polyethylene) pipe, which is resistant to corrosion and leaks. It's an effective long-term fix for widespread issues in Lubbock, TX.

    Sectional Point Repair in Lubbock

    When damage is confined to a specific section of pipe, sectional point repair is an ideal trenchless solution. We target only the compromised area, installing a patch or liner to seal the leak or reinforce the pipe. This method is cost-effective and minimizes the scope of work, perfect for isolated issues in Lubbock without affecting the entire line.

    Trenchless pipe repairs, particularly CIPP lining and pipe bursting using materials like epoxy resins and HDPE pipe, are designed to last for 50 years or more. These materials are highly resistant to corrosion, root intrusion, and chemical degradation, offering a very durable solution.

    Trenchless pipe repair is a complex process that requires specialized equipment, training, and expertise. It is not a DIY project.     Most trenchless pipe repair projects, especially CIPP lining, can be completed in one to three days. Pipe bursting might take a bit longer, depending on the pipe length and complexity. This is significantly faster than traditional methods, which can take weeks due to excavation and restoration.
