    Traditional Trench Sewer Line Replacement in Austin

    For significant damage or specific pipe configurations, traditional trenching may be the most effective method. This involves excavating a trench to access and replace the damaged sewer line directly. Our team carefully plans the excavation to minimize disruption to your landscape, utilizing advanced equipment for efficient and precise digging. We ensure proper grading and backfilling to prevent future issues and restore your property.

    Trenchless Sewer Line Repair & Replacement in Austin, TX

    Trenchless technology, such as pipe bursting or pipe lining, offers a less invasive alternative to traditional methods. Pipe bursting involves pulling a new pipe through the old one, fracturing the old pipe in place. Pipe lining inserts a new, durable epoxy-coated liner into the existing pipe. Both methods significantly reduce the need for extensive excavation, preserving your yard and landscaping in Austin, TX while providing a long-lasting solution.
