    Frequently Asked Questions About Residential Plumbing Service in Austin

    The cost for residential plumbing service in Austin varies widely depending on the type and complexity of the job. Minor repairs like a leaky faucet might range from $150-$350, while a water heater installation could be $1,200-$3,500. Major projects like slab leak repair or whole-home repiping can cost several thousand dollars. We always provide a detailed, upfront quote after diagnosing the issue.

    Hard water contains high levels of dissolved minerals, primarily calcium and magnesium, common in Austin's water supply. Soft water has these minerals removed. Hard water can cause mineral buildup in pipes, reduced soap lather, and appliance damage, while soft water is gentler on plumbing and skin.

    We work with all major reputable brands for plumbing fixtures and water heaters, including Moen, Kohler, Delta, Rheem, A.O. Smith, Bradford White, and Navien. Our technicians are trained to service and install a wide variety of models, ensuring compatibility and quality for your home.

    While some minor clogs or leaky faucets can be DIY projects, most residential plumbing repairs are best left to professionals. Incorrectly performed repairs can lead to more significant damage, costly leaks, or even safety hazards, especially with gas lines or water heaters. For durable, code-compliant solutions in Austin, always call a licensed plumber.

    For significant plumbing work like water heater replacement, sewer line repair, or new gas line installations in Austin, TX, a permit is typically required by local code. DFJ Plumbing handles all necessary permit applications and ensures all work complies with city regulations, saving you time and hassle.

    We recommend having your water heater inspected annually, especially in Austin where hard water can accelerate sediment buildup. Regular inspections can extend the lifespan of your unit, improve efficiency, and help identify potential issues like anode rod corrosion or pressure relief valve problems before they become major repairs.

    A slab leak is a leak in the water pipes located beneath your home's concrete foundation. They are common in Texas due to shifting clay soils, which can put stress on pipes, and the prevalence of homes built on slab foundations. Signs include warm spots on the floor, sudden increases in water bills, or the sound of running water when no fixtures are in use.

    Common signs that you need professional drain cleaning include slow drains, gurgling sounds from your pipes, foul odors coming from drains, and water backing up in sinks or showers. If you're experiencing any of these issues in your Austin home, it's time to call a professional before the clog worsens.
