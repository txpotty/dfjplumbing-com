    Our team uses advanced electronic listening devices to pinpoint the exact location of a slab leak.     Thermal Imaging for Slab Leaks in Irving, TX

    Thermal imaging cameras allow us to visualize temperature differences on surfaces, which can indicate a hidden water leak. As water escapes a hot water line, it creates a distinct thermal signature on the concrete slab. This technique is particularly useful for hot water line leaks and helps us narrow down the search area significantly without any physical disruption to your property in Irving.

    Pressure Testing & Isolation in Irving

    When a leak is suspected, we can perform pressure tests on individual water lines.     Frequently Asked Questions About Slab Leak Detection in Irving

    The cost for professional slab leak detection in Irving, TX typically ranges from $250 to $700, depending on the complexity of the leak and the time required for diagnosis. This initial detection fee is separate from any repair costs, which can vary widely based on the leak's location and required pipe repair.

    In Texas, common pipe materials prone to slab leaks include older copper pipes susceptible to corrosion from hard water or soil acidity, and sometimes PEX or CPVC if they were improperly installed or exposed to extreme temperatures. Galvanized steel pipes, though less common under slabs today, can also corrode and leak.

    A water line slab leak involves pressurized fresh water escaping from supply pipes, often resulting in higher water bills, warm spots, or the sound of running water. A sewer line slab leak involves wastewater leaking from drain pipes, which typically causes foul odors, slow drains, or visible sewage seepage, and is not usually pressurized.

    We stand by the accuracy of our slab leak detection services. While detection itself is a diagnostic service, we ensure our findings are precise. Any subsequent repairs performed by DFJ Plumbing come with our standard labor and parts warranties, which will be clearly outlined for you.

    Most slab leak detection services can be completed within 1 to 3 hours, depending on the severity and location of the leak. Our goal is always to be as efficient and non-invasive as possible, providing a quick diagnosis so repair work can begin promptly.

    Common signs of a slab leak in Texas homes include unexplained increases in water bills, warm spots on your floor, the sound of running water when no fixtures are in use, damp or mildewy smells, and visible cracks in your foundation or walls. Pooling water outside your home near the foundation can also be a strong indicator.

    While you can look for the signs, accurately detecting a slab leak requires specialized equipment and expertise that most homeowners don't possess. DIY attempts often lead to more damage or misdiagnosis, making professional detection a more cost-effective and reliable solution in the long run.

    Many homeowner insurance policies cover the cost of water damage from a sudden or accidental slab leak, and sometimes the cost of accessing the leak for repair. However, coverage varies greatly, so it's essential to check your specific policy with your insurance provider in Irving, TX.
