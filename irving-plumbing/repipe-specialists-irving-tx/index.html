    Whole-Home Copper Repiping in Irving

    Copper piping offers exceptional durability, corrosion resistance, and a long lifespan, making it a premium choice for many homeowners in Irving. While the upfront cost is higher than PEX, copper provides superior rigidity and resistance to UV light if exposed. Our team meticulously solders each joint, ensuring a leak-proof and robust plumbing system that can withstand the test of time, including Texas's variable temperatures and water conditions. Copper also naturally inhibits bacterial growth, contributing to cleaner water.

    Efficient PEX Repiping in Irving, TX

    PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) is a flexible, cost-effective, and highly popular choice for repiping projects in Irving, TX. Its flexibility allows for fewer connections, reducing potential leak points and making installation quicker and less intrusive than traditional rigid pipes. PEX is also highly resistant to freezing, scaling, and chlorine, common issues in Texas water. We use high-quality PEX-A or PEX-B tubing, installed with durable crimp or expansion fittings, to create a reliable and efficient plumbing network throughout your home.

    Slab Leak Repiping Solutions in Irving

    Slab leaks are a frequent and damaging problem for homes built on concrete foundations in Irving. These leaks often require rerouting or repiping entire sections of your plumbing system to bypass the compromised pipes under the slab. Our repipe specialists use advanced detection tools to pinpoint slab leaks and then strategically reroute new PEX or copper lines through your attic or walls, completely bypassing the problematic underground sections. This prevents further foundation damage and restores water service without breaking your slab.

    Galvanized Pipe Replacement in Irving, TX

    Many older homes in Irving, TX still have galvanized steel pipes, which are prone to corrosion, rust, and mineral buildup. This leads to low water pressure, discolored water, and eventual leaks.
