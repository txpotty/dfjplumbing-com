    Anode Rod Inspection & Replacement in San Angelo, TX

    The anode rod is your hot water tank's sacrificial protector against corrosion, and it's especially important with the mineral content often found in Texas water. During our maintenance, we pull and inspect the anode rod. If it's significantly depleted, we recommend and perform a replacement to extend the life of your tank in San Angelo, TX, preventing rust from eating away at the tank lining.

    Pressure Relief Valve Testing in San Angelo

    A properly functioning temperature and pressure relief (T&P) valve is a crucial safety device for any hot water tank.
