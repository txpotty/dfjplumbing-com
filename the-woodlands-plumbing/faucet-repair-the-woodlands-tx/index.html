    Cartridge Faucet Repair in The Woodlands, TX

    Many modern single-handle faucets, commonly found in newer Texas homes, use a cartridge system.     Compression Faucet Repair in The Woodlands

    Compression faucets, often found in older The Woodlands homes, are known for their separate hot and cold handles. When these faucets leak, it's typically due to worn-out rubber washers or packing. Our team is skilled in disassembling these classic fixtures, replacing the necessary seals and washers, and reassembling them to stop leaks effectively.     Ball Faucet Repair in The Woodlands, TX

    Ball faucets, recognizable by their single lever that moves over a rounded cap, are common in many Texas kitchens. They use a rotating ball joint to control water flow and temperature. If your ball faucet is leaking from the spout or base, or if the handle feels loose, it's likely time for a repair. We can replace the spring-loaded rubber seats and O-rings, which are usually the culprits, restoring smooth operation and preventing drips.

    Disc Faucet Repair in The Woodlands

While generally reliable, they can develop leaks if the ceramic discs become scratched or misaligned, or if the inlet and outlet seals wear out. Our plumbers have the expertise to carefully disassemble these precise mechanisms, clean or replace the ceramic discs, and reseal the faucet to eliminate leaks and restore optimal performance.
