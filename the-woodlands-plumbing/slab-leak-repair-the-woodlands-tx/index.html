    Direct Slab Leak Repair in The Woodlands, TX

    When a slab leak is localized and accessible, direct repair is often the most efficient solution.     Slab Leak Pipe Rerouting in The Woodlands

    For more complex or recurring slab leaks, or when the original piping is severely deteriorated, pipe rerouting is an excellent long-term solution. This method involves abandoning the leaky pipe under the slab and installing new plumbing lines through walls or the attic, bypassing the foundation entirely. This eliminates future slab leak risks from that particular line and is often preferred for older homes in The Woodlands with aging copper or galvanized pipes.

    PEX Repiping for Slab Leaks in The Woodlands, TX

    If multiple slab leaks indicate a systemic issue with your home's original plumbing, a full PEX repipe might be the most cost-effective and durable solution. PEX tubing is flexible, corrosion-resistant, and less prone to leaks caused by foundation shifts, making it ideal for Texas homes. We can replace all the water supply lines in your The Woodlands home, providing a brand-new, leak-free plumbing system that will last for decades.
