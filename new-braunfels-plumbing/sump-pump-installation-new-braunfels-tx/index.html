    Primary Sump Pump Installation in New Braunfels

    A primary sump pump is your main defense against basement flooding. We install robust, electrically powered pumps designed to automatically activate when water levels rise in the sump pit. Our plumbers will assess your basement's water intrusion risks, considering local water tables and soil types common in New Braunfels, to recommend the optimal pump size and horsepower for effective water removal.

    Battery Backup Sump Pump Installation in New Braunfels, TX

    Power outages during heavy storms are a real concern in Texas, which is why a battery backup sump pump is a smart addition. This system automatically takes over if your primary pump loses power, ensuring continuous flood protection. We install reliable backup units that can operate for hours, giving you peace of mind even when the electricity goes out in New Braunfels.

    Combination Sump Pump Systems in New Braunfels

    For ultimate protection, consider a combination sump pump system which integrates both a primary and a battery backup pump into one unit. These systems are pre-assembled for easier installation and offer a compact solution for flood prevention. DFJ Plumbing can install these high-efficiency units, ensuring your New Braunfels home has dual layers of defense against water damage.

    Pedestal Sump Pump Installation in New Braunfels, TX

    Pedestal sump pumps have their motor mounted above the sump pit, making them easier to access for maintenance and repair. They are often a good choice for shallow sump pits or areas where space is a concern. Our team is skilled in installing these types of pumps, ensuring proper pit depth and discharge line setup for optimal performance in New Braunfels, TX.

    Submersible Sump Pump Installation in New Braunfels

    Submersible sump pumps sit directly inside the sump pit, operating quietly and taking up less space. They are typically more powerful and durable, making them a popular choice for many homeowners. We handle the complete installation of submersible units, including proper sealing and electrical connections, tailored to the specific needs of your New Braunfels property.

    While DIY sump pump installation is possible, it is not recommended.     To keep your new sump pump running efficiently, it is recommended to test it monthly by pouring water into the pit to ensure it activates.     A submersible sump pump sits entirely inside the sump pit, submerged in water, making it quieter and taking up less space.
