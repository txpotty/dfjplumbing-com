    Type K, L, and M copper pipes differ primarily in their wall thickness and thus their pressure ratings. Type K has the thickest walls and is used for underground service and main water lines.     To prevent future copper pipe leaks, consider installing a whole-house water softener if you have hard water, as mineral buildup can lead to corrosion.
