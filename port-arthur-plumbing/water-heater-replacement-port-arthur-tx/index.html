    Most standard tank-style water heater replacements can be completed in 3 to 5 hours, assuming no major complications or plumbing reconfigurations. Tankless or hybrid installations might take a bit longer, often 5 to 8 hours, due to more complex venting or gas line adjustments.

    Yes, in many areas of Port Arthur and Texas, a plumbing permit is required for water heater replacement to ensure the installation meets local safety and building codes. DFJ Plumbing handles all necessary permitting processes on your behalf, so you don't have to worry about compliance.

    A traditional water heater stores and heats a limited amount of water in a tank, whereas a tankless water heater heats water on demand as it flows through the unit.     Most new water heaters come with a manufacturer's warranty ranging from 6 to 12 years on the tank and components, depending on the model and brand. DFJ Plumbing also provides a labor warranty on our installation services, ensuring your investment is protected.

    Common signs include a lack of hot water, rust-colored water, metallic taste in hot water, strange noises like rumbling or popping from the tank, visible leaks around the base of the unit, or an old water heater that is over 10-12 years old.
