    Leaky Faucet Repair in Cedar Park

    A dripping faucet is more than just an annoyance, it's a waste of water and money. In Texas, where water conservation is always a concern, fixing a leak quickly is important.     Cartridge Faucet Repair in Cedar Park, TX

    Many modern single-handle faucets, commonly found in newer Texas homes, use a cartridge system. When these fail, you might experience inconsistent water temperature, reduced flow, or difficulty turning the faucet on or off. Our technicians are experts at diagnosing cartridge issues and replacing them with the correct manufacturer-specific part, ensuring your faucet functions like new. We work with all major brands to provide precise repairs.

    Compression Faucet Repair in Cedar Park

    Compression faucets, often found in older Cedar Park homes, are known for their separate hot and cold handles. When these faucets leak, it's typically due to worn-out rubber washers or packing. Our team is skilled in disassembling these classic fixtures, replacing the necessary seals and washers, and reassembling them to stop leaks effectively. We respect the character of older homes and ensure these repairs are done carefully.

    Ball Faucet Repair in Cedar Park, TX

    Ball faucets, recognizable by their single lever that moves over a rounded cap, are common in many Texas kitchens. They use a rotating ball joint to control water flow and temperature. If your ball faucet is leaking from the spout or base, or if the handle feels loose, it's likely time for a repair. We can replace the spring-loaded rubber seats and O-rings, which are usually the culprits, restoring smooth operation and preventing drips.

    Disc Faucet Repair in Cedar Park

    Ceramic disc faucets are known for their durability and are a popular choice in many contemporary Cedar Park bathrooms. While generally reliable, they can develop leaks if the ceramic discs become scratched or misaligned, or if the inlet and outlet seals wear out. Our plumbers have the expertise to carefully disassemble these precise mechanisms, clean or replace the ceramic discs, and reseal the faucet to eliminate leaks and restore optimal performance.

    The cost of faucet repair in Cedar Park can range from $120 to $350, depending on the type of faucet, the specific parts needed, and the complexity of the repair.     The most common signs include a persistent drip or leak, difficulty turning the handles, inconsistent water temperature, reduced water pressure from the faucet, or strange noises when operating it. Any of these could indicate internal component wear or damage requiring attention.

    Our plumbers are experienced in repairing all major faucet brands commonly found in Texas homes, including Delta, Moen, Kohler, American Standard, Pfister, and many others. We keep a stock of universal and common brand-specific parts to expedite repairs.

    Yes, DFJ Plumbing stands behind our work. We offer a warranty on both the parts we install and our labor for faucet repairs. Specific warranty details will be provided at the time of service, giving you peace of mind that your repair is covered.

    Faucet repair involves fixing the existing fixture by replacing specific worn or broken internal components like washers, O-rings, or cartridges. Faucet replacement means removing the old faucet entirely and installing a brand new one. We recommend replacement when a faucet is severely damaged, very old, or if you simply want an upgrade.

    Most standard faucet repairs, such as replacing a washer or cartridge, can be completed by an experienced plumber in about 30 minutes to an hour. More complex issues or specialty faucets might take longer, but we strive for efficient service to minimize disruption to your home in Cedar Park.

    For a standard faucet repair, where only internal components are replaced and the fixture itself remains, no plumbing permits are typically required in Cedar Park.     Texas is known for its hard water, which contains high levels of minerals like calcium and magnesium.
