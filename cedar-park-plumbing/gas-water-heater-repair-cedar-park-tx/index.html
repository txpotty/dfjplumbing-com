    Frequently Asked Questions About Gas Water Heater Repair in Cedar Park

    The cost of gas water heater repair in Cedar Park, TX can vary widely depending on the issue. Minor repairs like a thermocouple replacement might range from $150-$350, while more complex issues such as a gas valve replacement could be $400-$800 or more. We always provide a detailed, upfront estimate after diagnosis.

    A thermocouple is a safety device in a gas water heater that senses if the pilot light is lit. If it doesn't detect heat, it shuts off the gas supply to prevent gas leaks. A faulty thermocouple is a very common reason for a pilot light not staying lit and is a critical safety component.

    Yes, our technicians are trained and experienced in repairing all major brands of gas water heaters commonly found in Cedar Park, TX homes, including Rheem, Bradford White, A.O. Smith, State, Noritz, and more. We carry a wide array of universal and brand-specific parts.

    Generally, minor repairs like replacing a thermocouple or a thermostat do not require a permit. However, if the repair involves significant alterations to the gas line or venting system, or if the unit needs to be replaced, a permit from the Cedar Park building department might be necessary. We handle all necessary permitting.

    Regular maintenance, such as annual flushing of the tank to remove sediment, inspecting the anode rod, and checking the pressure relief valve, can significantly extend the life of your gas water heater and prevent common issues. Consider a maintenance plan with DFJ Plumbing.

    If you smell gas, immediately evacuate your home, do not turn on or off any lights or appliances, and call your gas company's emergency line and then DFJ Plumbing from a safe location. This indicates a potentially dangerous gas leak that requires immediate professional attention.

    Look out for a lack of hot water, inconsistent water temperature, a pilot light that won't stay lit, unusual noises like rumbling or popping, a rotten egg smell (indicating a gas leak), or visible leaks around the tank. Any of these warrant a professional inspection.

    Due to the inherent risks associated with natural gas and potential carbon monoxide poisoning, gas water heater repairs should always be handled by a licensed professional. DIY attempts can be dangerous and may void warranties or violate local Cedar Park, TX plumbing codes.
