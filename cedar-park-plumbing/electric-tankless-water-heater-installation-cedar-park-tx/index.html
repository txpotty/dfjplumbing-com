    Yes, electric tankless water heaters typically require dedicated high-amperage circuits, often 200 amps or more, depending on the unit's size and your home's hot water demand. Many older Cedar Park, TX homes may need an electrical panel upgrade to accommodate these requirements. Our plumbers will assess your current electrical system during the consultation.

    With proper installation and regular maintenance, an electric tankless water heater can last 20 years or more, which is significantly longer than the 10-15 year lifespan of a traditional tank unit. Their modular design also means components can often be replaced, extending their service life further.

    In Texas, electric tankless water heaters offer endless hot water, significant energy savings by heating only on demand, and a compact size that saves valuable space. They also avoid standby heat loss, which is particularly beneficial in our warmer climate, contributing to lower utility bills year-round.

    Installing an electric tankless water heater is a complex job involving high-voltage electricity and plumbing, making it unsuitable for DIY.     Texas is known for its hard water, which can lead to mineral buildup (scale) inside electric tankless water heaters. This can reduce efficiency and lifespan.
