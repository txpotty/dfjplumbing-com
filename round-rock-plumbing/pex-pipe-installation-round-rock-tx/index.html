    Signs you might need PEX repiping include consistently low water pressure, discolored or rusty water, frequent leaks, or if your home has old galvanized steel or polybutylene pipes. These issues are common in older Round Rock homes and indicate a failing plumbing system.

    We install high-quality PEX piping from reputable manufacturers such as Uponor (PEX-A), Viega (PEX-B), and Zurn (PEX-B). We also work with other trusted brands like SharkBite for fittings, ensuring the best materials are used for your specific installation.

    PEX can be used for outdoor plumbing applications like irrigation lines or outdoor spigots, but it needs to be protected from direct UV light exposure, which can degrade the material over time. When used outdoors, PEX should always be buried or otherwise shielded.

    Yes, PEX piping is certified safe for drinking water. It does not leach chemicals into water and is approved by all major plumbing codes for potable water systems. It's a healthier alternative to older pipes that can corrode and affect water quality.

    While PEX is simpler to install than copper, professional installation is highly recommended. Correct sizing, routing, and secure connections are crucial for preventing leaks and ensuring system longevity. Improper DIY installation can lead to costly water damage and code violations.
