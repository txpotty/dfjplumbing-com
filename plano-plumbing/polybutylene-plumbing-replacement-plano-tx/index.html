    Understanding Polybutylene Failure in Plano

    Polybutylene's inherent weakness stems from its chemical reaction with oxidants like chlorine, commonly used in Plano's municipal water treatment. Over time, this reaction causes the pipes and their plastic fittings to become brittle, leading to hairline cracks and eventual rupture. Texas's harder water can also exacerbate this degradation. Understanding this failure mechanism is crucial for diagnosing issues and recommending the most effective and permanent replacement strategy for your home.
