    To prevent future freezing in Plano, insulate exposed pipes, especially those in attics, crawl spaces, and exterior walls. Disconnect garden hoses and drain outdoor spigots.     Common signs of a frozen pipe include a sudden drop in water pressure, no water coming from a specific faucet, visible frost on an exposed pipe, or strange gurgling sounds from your drains.     Texas plumbing codes, while generally less stringent on freeze protection than northern states, do require proper insulation for pipes in unconditioned spaces and minimum burial depths for water lines to reduce freezing risk.     While no pipe is completely freeze-proof, certain materials like PEX (cross-linked polyethylene) are more flexible and resistant to bursting when frozen compared to rigid copper or PVC.
