    Clogged Grease Trap Repair in League City

    Grease traps often clog due to excessive accumulation of fats, oils, and grease (FOGs) or food solids. Identifying the precise location and nature of the clog is crucial for effective repair and long-term performance in your League City establishment.

    Leaking Grease Trap Repair in League City, TX

    A leaking grease trap can cause significant property damage, foul odors, and potential health code violations. Leaks often stem from corroded tanks, cracked seams, or failing pipe connections. We use leak detection techniques to pinpoint the source and provide targeted repairs, which may involve patching the tank, sealing connections, or replacing damaged sections of the trap or associated plumbing. Addressing leaks promptly is essential to maintain a sanitary environment and avoid costly remediation in League City, TX.

    Damaged Baffle Repair in League City

    The baffles inside a grease trap are critical for separating FOGs from wastewater. If these baffles are corroded, broken, or dislodged, the trap's efficiency plummets, allowing grease into the sewer system. Our service includes inspecting the condition of all baffles and repairing or replacing them as needed, often using durable, corrosion-resistant materials. Proper baffle function is key to compliance with wastewater regulations in League City.
