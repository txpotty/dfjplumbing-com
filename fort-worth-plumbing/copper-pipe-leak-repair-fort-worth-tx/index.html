    The cost for copper pipe leak repair in Fort Worth varies widely, typically ranging from $200 for a minor, accessible pinhole leak to over $2,000 for complex repairs like under-slab leaks requiring extensive work.     Copper pipe leaks are often caused by corrosion, especially from hard or acidic water common in parts of Texas. Other causes include excessive water pressure, improper installation, shifting foundations putting stress on joints, and occasional freezing temperatures that can cause pipes to burst. Pinhole leaks are a common symptom of internal corrosion.

    While temporary fixes like clamps or epoxy putties exist, professional repair is highly recommended for lasting results. DIY repairs often fail, leading to more significant water damage and costly secondary issues.     To prevent future copper pipe leaks, consider installing a whole-house water softener if you have hard water, as mineral buildup can lead to corrosion. Maintaining appropriate water pressure, ensuring proper pipe insulation during cold weather, and scheduling periodic plumbing inspections can also significantly extend the life of your copper pipes.

    Type K, L, and M copper pipes differ primarily in their wall thickness and thus their pressure ratings. Type K has the thickest walls and is used for underground service and main water lines.
